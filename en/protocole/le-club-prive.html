The private members' club

A private club is a house whose rules are a contract: the House Rules are published, accepted upon entry, enforced without exception. They all say the same thing: what happens at the club stays at the club.

What the institutions publish

  • Soho House. Any photography or filming, including with a telephone, is forbidden inside the Houses of Soho House.
  • Soho House. A member, limited to three guests, may neither part from them inside the club nor leave them there after departing.
  • Casa Cipriani New York. After 18:00, members and guests are asked to keep their telephones on silent and to refrain from making calls or using a laptop, with text messages remaining permitted and calls possible in the club's designated areas.
  • Casa Cipriani New York. Those under 21 are not admitted to the club, except at weekend brunch, accompanied by a member in the designated areas.
  • The Arts Club. Laptops, telephones, tablets and work papers are tolerated only until 17:00, and never in the dining rooms or in the Oscuro.
  • Debrett's. One never tips a club's staff: to reward them, there is generally a common fund called the Holiday Box, whose contributions are shared among all employees.

Each rule is taken from the document published by the institution, consulted at its official address on 27/08/2026.

Custom, according to the house

One never asks who was there. One does not introduce oneself to a public figure: one waits for a member to make the introductions. One addresses the staff by name and with consideration. Being a guest is not being a member: one follows, one does not lead.
