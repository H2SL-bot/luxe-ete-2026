September in Venice

The first red carpet of the season unrolls on the Lido: ten days of the Mostra, the amfAR gala as its crowning night, and an entire lagoon dressed for a premiere. The art palazzi stay open throughout the festival, and the grand Carnival ball is already taking reservations.

  • 2-12 September 2026 · Palazzo del Cinema, Lido, Venice
    Venice Film Festival 2026 — 83rd Venice International Film Festival
    The Lido rolls out its red carpet: the Golden Lion, cinema's elite and yachts on the lagoon.
  • Sunday, September 6, 2026 · Molino Stucky, Venice
    amfAR Gala Venezia 2026 — 6th Edition
    The Mostra's black-tie gala: stars, collectors and Marina Abramović honored at the Molino Stucky, sponsored by Chopard.
  • 9 May - 23 November 2026 (open in August)
    Helter Skelter: Arthur Jafa and Richard Prince (Fondazione Prada Venice)
    Palazzo Prada on the Grand Canal, Jafa and Prince on show, the Prada ecosystem, public access.
