The Addresses

The places that do not depend on a date: clubs, beach clubs, dinner shows. How to get in, what it costs, who to write to.

In Paris, two institutions are earned rather than reserved: Silencio, a private club joined by application, and venues of the Hôtel Costes or Caves du Roy kind, part stage, part salon, whose ways in are detailed in the radar's guides, on the home page.
