Monte-Carlo Summer Festival - dinner-shows at the Salle des Étoiles

August 2026 (until 15 August: LP on the 1st, SOUL! from the 2nd to the 9th, Lisa Stansfield on the 11th, Laura Pausini on the 15th) · Monaco · Palace Hotels & Fine Dining

The August fortnight of the Monte-Carlo Summer Festival (3 July-15 August 2026) at the Sporting's Salle des Étoiles: glamorous open-air dinner-shows, the simplest gateway into Monegasque evenings in August. Closing with Laura Pausini on 15 August.

Line-up : LP (1 August), the SOUL! show (2-9 August), Lisa Stansfield (11 August), Laura Pausini (15 August)

Access

Direct booking: montecarlosbm.com, +377 98 06 36 36. Dinner-show package from 420 EUR (LP, Lisa Stansfield); less expensive seats depending on the show. Elegant dress code required.

The turnkey stay

Settle into the heart of Larvotto, just steps from the Salle des Étoiles, where the roof opens to the stars as The Last Dinner Party, Aya Nakamura, John Legend and Vanessa Paradis take the stage. Here, an exceptional evening does not end with the final encore: it flows into a three-star dinner facing the Mediterranean, a night in a palace hotel at the water's edge, then a morning at the Thermes Marins and on the private beach. A Monegasque art de vivre in which every moment, from the concert to daybreak, is orchestrated like a show.

Where to stay

  • Monte-Carlo Bay Hotel & Resort — A contemporary palace hotel on a 4-hectare peninsula at 40 Avenue Princesse Grace, just a few hundred metres from the Salle des Étoiles: a turquoise sand-bottom lagoon, the Spa Clarins & Myblend and sea views. The closest address to the Sporting, an easy walk home after the concert.
  • Hôtel de Paris Monte-Carlo — A legendary palace hotel dating from 1864 on the Place du Casino, a showcase of the art de vivre signed by the Société des Bains de Mer, with its starred restaurants and fabled wine cellars. Minutes from the Sporting, for an exceptional night in the Principality's most emblematic setting.

Where to dine

  • Le Louis XV – Alain Ducasse à l'Hôtel de Paris — Alain Ducasse and Emmanuel Pilon's three-starred table, the absolute benchmark of Mediterranean haute cuisine in a Belle Époque setting. The ideal fine-dining sequel to prolong the magic of the Salle des Étoiles (jacket required at dinner).
  • Blue Bay Marcel Ravin — Chef Marcel Ravin's two-Michelin-starred restaurant at the Monte-Carlo Bay, a bold fusion of his native Martinique and the Mediterranean, with a terrace open to the sea and a homegrown organic kitchen garden. The Sporting's immediate neighbour: dinner and concert in the same setting.

What to experience

  • Thermes Marins Monte-Carlo — A 6,600 m² temple of well-being facing the Mediterranean, with a seawater pool drawn from 77 m deep, two hammams, a jacuzzi, a solarium and signature treatments. The perfect restorative interlude the morning after a night at the Salle des Étoiles.
  • Monte-Carlo Beach — The Société des Bains de Mer's iconic private beach and beach club on Avenue Princesse Grace, with an Olympic pool, cabanas and the starred restaurant Elsa at the water's edge. An exceptional Mediterranean afternoon, moments from the Sporting.
