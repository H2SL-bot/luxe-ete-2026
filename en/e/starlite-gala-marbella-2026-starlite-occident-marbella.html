Starlite Gala Marbella 2026 (Starlite Occident)

Gala evening · Cantera de Nagüeles, Marbella (exact date to be reconfirmed) · Cantera de Nagüeles, Marbella · Palace Hotels & Fine Dining
✓ Verified at the source on 17/07/2026

The great charity gala of the Andalusian summer: co-chaired by Antonio Banderas and Sandra García-Sanjuán, the Starlite Gala brings together celebrities, industrialists and all of Marbella's high society at the Cantera de Nagüeles for a dinner-auction in aid of the Starlite Foundation and CUDECA.

Line-up : Antonio Banderas and Sandra García-Sanjuán (co-chairs), celebrities and Marbella's high society

How to get in

Starlite Occident, organiser of the Starlite Gala at the Cantera de Nagüeles (Marbella), in aid of the Starlite Foundation and CUDECA

Two options: buy a ticket or a dinner-auction table through the official ticket office (entradas.starlitemarbella.com), or request an invitation via the organisation (+34 664 152 513). A high-end charity evening with limited capacity: book early. Confirm the exact date of the gala evening with the ticket office.

The turnkey stay

La Cantera de Nagüeles — an open-air quarry ringed by high rock walls at the foot of La Concha — hosts the Gala Starlite every summer, the most coveted charity night of the Marbella season. Co-chaired by Antonio Banderas and Sandra García-Sanjuán, president of the foundation, the evening brings the international jet set together in aid of the Fundación Starlite's charitable projects, at the heart of the Starlite Occident 2026 season, which this year features Zucchero, Ozuna, Nile Rodgers & Chic and Miguel Ríos. Guests arrive as neighbours from the Golden Mile, some ten minutes' drive away, to dine, bid and dance beneath the Andalusian stars — the exact date of the 2026 edition is to be confirmed with the organisers.

Where to stay

  • Marbella Club Hotel · Golf Resort & Spa — The founding palace of the Golden Mile (1954), on boulevard Príncipe Alfonso von Hohenlohe, about ten minutes by car from La Cantera de Nagüeles. Seafront gardens, a spa devoted to holistic rituals and a beach club with its emblematic palapa, where fish and shellfish come straight to the table.
  • Puente Romano Beach Resort — A five-star Andalusian village by the sea on the Golden Mile, at the foot of the Nagüeles district. More than twenty restaurants — Nobu, COYA, Cipriani —, a legendary tennis club and the Six Senses Spa.

Where to dine

  • Skina — Two Michelin stars in Marbella (Avda. Cánovas del Castillo 9). Chef-owner Marcos Granda's intimate dining room celebrates a contemporary Andalusia, carried by local seasonal produce and a remarkable cellar.
  • Messina — One Michelin star (2026 guide), avenida Severo Ochoa 12. Since 2003, Argentine chef Mauricio Giovanini and Pía Ninci, head of the dining room, have served tasting menus poised between the Mediterranean and Latin America, with a chef's table in the kitchen.

What to experience

  • Vol privé en hélicoptère, HeliAir Marbella — The Costa del Sol's long-standing operator (since 1988), with its heliport at Puerto Banús (carretera de Istán, near the Manolo Santana Racquet Club): panoramic flights along the coast and private VIP charters in a Bell 505 — the arrival that sets the tone before the Gala.
  • Yacht privé au départ de Puerto Banús, Lovit Charter — A day at sea or a sunset cruise aboard a wholly private yacht — up to the Ferretti 680 — from pantalán 3 at Puerto José Banús, professional skipper included.
