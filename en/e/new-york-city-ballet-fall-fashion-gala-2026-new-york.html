New York City Ballet — Fall Fashion Gala 2026

Thursday 1 October 2026 · David H. Koch Theater, Lincoln Center · David H. Koch Theater, Lincoln Center, New York · Fashion & Couture

Le gala qui marie la danse et la haute couture : trois premières mondiales, avec des costumes signés Robert Wun, Viktor&Rolf et Simone Rocha, au David H. Koch Theater du Lincoln Center. Cocktail, spectacle, puis dîner et bal sur la Promenade.

Line-up : NYC Ballet principal dancers, fashion designers, donors

How to get in

New York City Ballet, David H. Koch Theater, Lincoln Center

Gala by invitation/patronage. Direct route: a performance ticket via nycballet.com; for the gala dinner, contact the Special Events office or become a supporting donor. Evening dress.

The turnkey stay

On 1 October 2026, New York City Ballet turns the David H. Koch Theater into a runway: Ruinart champagne cocktails from 17:30, three world premieres — Jennifer Archibald, Alysa Pires and a pas de deux by Justin Peck costumed by Simone Rocha — then dinner and dancing on the theatre's Promenade, beneath the chandeliers of Lincoln Center. The Fall Fashion Gala, sponsored by Wells Fargo for the sixth consecutive year, remains the evening where New York fashion and dance become one — black tie, designer gowns and the company's principals on the same stage. The perfect round trip for anyone who knows how to pair Manhattan with autumn.

Where to stay

  • Mandarin Oriental, New York — Perched on floors 35 to 54 of the Deutsche Bank Center on Columbus Circle, a few minutes' walk from Lincoln Center. Sweeping views over Central Park and the Hudson, an exceptional spa and a 23-metre indoor pool: the address closest to the gala, so you can walk back in evening dress.
  • Park Hyatt New York — At 153 West 57th Street, facing Carnegie Hall, about ten minutes from the Koch Theater. Five Forbes stars for ten consecutive years, architecture by Christian de Portzamparc, and the Spa Nalai on the 25th floor with its salt-water pool overlooking the Manhattan skyline.

Where to dine

  • Per Se — Thomas Keller's New York masterpiece, three Michelin stars without interruption since 2006, at 10 Columbus Circle with views over Central Park. A tasting menu in nine acts and a cellar of more than 2,000 references, a few minutes' walk from the Koch Theater — ideal the evening before the gala.
  • Jean-Georges — Jean-Georges Vongerichten's flagship, two Michelin stars, at 1 Central Park West, steps from Lincoln Center. Floor-to-ceiling windows onto the park, French technique with Asian accents: the Upper West Side's institution for an elegant lunch.

What to experience

  • Transfert hélicoptère BLADE JFK — Manhattan — A five-minute flight between JFK and the Manhattan heliports, private lounges at departure and a car to the terminal: the only way to arrive at the gala without ever meeting a New York traffic jam.
  • Visite des coulisses du Metropolitan Opera — On the same plaza as the Koch Theater, the Met opens its scenery workshops, rehearsal rooms and dressing rooms on official guided tours of about 75 minutes. The schedule depends on rehearsals (some weekday afternoons at 15:00): book ahead, ideally the day before or the afternoon of the gala if a tour is scheduled.
