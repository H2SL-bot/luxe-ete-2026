BIG ART Festival — Robbie Williams live

Sunday 9 August 2026 (8 PM cocktail, 9 PM gala dinner, 10 PM show, 10:50 PM Robbie Williams live, 12:50 AM DJ set) · Romazzino, A Belmond Hotel : Costa Smeralda · Festivals & Music

A night written in the stars of Sardinia: in the gardens of the Romazzino Belmond, cocktails at sunset then a gala dinner overlooking the sea before Robbie Williams's private concert beneath the palm trees. An intimate setting, premium tables, a show and an after DJ set — the spirit of a grand jet-set villa where you dance until the small hours.

Line-up : Robbie Williams (live performance confirmed)

How to get in

Attendance by request via the official portal events.bigart-festival.com; 3 published categories: GOLD 3 500 EUR, PLATINUM 4 000 EUR, BLACK 7 000 EUR/person, gala dinner + live performance + premium drinks at the table included. Dress code: evening attire. Limited places, RSVP required.

The turnkey stay

Set down your bags before the emerald waters of Romazzino and extend the magic of the Robbie Williams gala well beyond the 12:50 AM DJ set, without ever taking to the road. Wake up on the private beach, a signature Shiseido spa ritual, lunch with your feet in the sand then a Michelin-starred dinner on the old harbour of Porto Cervo: these few days turn an exceptional evening into a complete Sardinian art of living. A private escape by sea toward the coves of La Maddalena seals this turnkey stay, amid pink granite, fragrant maquis and the turquoise Mediterranean.

Where to stay

  • Romazzino, A Belmond Hotel, Costa Smeralda — The very palace of the gala: sleep where the evening unfolds. An icon designed by Michele Busiri Vici (1965), reopened after renovation, private sandy beach, four hectares of gardens, white architecture facing the emerald sea. The ultimate choice to extend the night without moving an inch.
  • Hotel Cala di Volpe, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Costa Smeralda — The founding grand palace of the Costa Smeralda (1963), minutes from Romazzino, on one of the island's most beautiful bays. 121 frescoed rooms and suites, an Olympic pool facing the open sea, a Shiseido Spa and legendary five-star service.

Where to dine

  • Italo Bassi Confusion Restaurant (1 étoile Michelin) — The gourmet table of Porto Cervo, on the Promenade du Port facing the Old Harbour. Contemporary cuisine by chef Italo Bassi, tasting menus or an à la carte of exceptional produce (oysters, caviar, raw catch of the sea), Bubble Bar & Bistrot on the lower floor.
  • Quattro Passi al Pescatore, Porto Cervo — The iconic waterside restaurant of the Vecchio Molo, the very first restaurant built on the Costa Smeralda (1960s, Jacques Couelle). Refined Sardinian cuisine in partnership with three-Michelin-star chef Antonio Mellino, dinner then an evening in an intimate atmosphere until 2 AM.

What to experience

  • Sortie en mer privée, Archipel de La Maddalena (Smeralda Boats) — A private day with a skipper departing from the marina of Porto Cervo toward the national park of La Maddalena: translucent coves, secret anchorages and southern Corsica within a sail's reach. Sailing yacht, catamaran or RIB, tailor-made.
  • Spa Shiseido, Hotel Cala di Volpe — A Japanese wellness ritual drawing on 140 years of expertise and the spirit of Omotenashi: signature face and body treatments, dedicated hammams, Spa Journeys scented with Kuroho. The perfect recovery the morning after the gala.
