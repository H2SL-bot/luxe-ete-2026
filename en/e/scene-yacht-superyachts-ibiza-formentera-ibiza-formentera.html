Ibiza-Formentera yacht & superyacht scene

July-August 2026 (peak season) · Mouillages Cala Jondal, Formentera (Illetes), Marina Ibiza / Botafoch · Other

July-August is the peak of the yachting scene: superyachts at anchor off Cala Jondal (in front of Blue Marlin) and Formentera, tenders to the beach clubs, lunches at Juan y Andrea / Beso Beach. The heart of Balearic nautical society (Portofino/yachting DNA). NB: not a ticketed event but a seasonal jet-set scene; commercial 'boat parties' (mass-market) remain out of scope.

Access

Access via private yacht/charter; beach club and beach restaurant bookings.

The turnkey stay

Anchor at Cala Jondal among the superyachts, lunch at the water's edge on Formentera, and return to a palace suite facing Dalt Vila.

Where to stay

  • Ibiza Gran Hotel — The island's only 5* Grand Luxe, all suites, on Marina Ibiza facing Dalt Vila and the superyachts of Botafoch.
  • Nobu Hotel Ibiza Bay — 5* in Talamanca with private mooring and tender, steps from Marina Botafoch; Six Senses spa and Nobu restaurant.

Where to dine

  • La Gaia by Óscar Molina — 1 Michelin star (2026), within the Ibiza Gran Hotel; Ibizan cuisine, signature tasting menus.
  • Juan y Andrea — The legendary table of Playa Illetes, Formentera, since 1971; tender from your yacht on VHF 74, grilled fish.

What to experience

  • Blue Marlin Ibiza — Iconic Cala Jondal beach club with a private pontoon for tenders; the epicentre of the south's superyacht scene.
  • Charter privé Ibiza, Formentera — A day on a private yacht, anchoring off Illetes, tenders to Juan y Andrea and the beach clubs.
