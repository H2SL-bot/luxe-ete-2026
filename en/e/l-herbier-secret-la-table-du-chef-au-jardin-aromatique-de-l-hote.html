L'Herbier Secret — The Chef's Table in the aromatic garden of the Hôtel de Crillon

From 12 June to 25 July, then from 27 August to 25 September 2026, Thursday to Saturday evenings. WARNING: CLOSED from 26 Jul… · Hôtel de Crillon, A Rosewood Hotel, 10 place de la Concorde, 75008 Paris — terrasse confidentielle du 2e étag… · Palace Hotels & Fine Dining

An ultra-confidential experience: a single table for 4 to 6 guests per evening in the palace's aromatic herb garden, a 5-course menu by executive chef Alan Taudon (plant- and sea-driven cuisine built around herbs grown on site), 250 EUR per guest, wine or alcohol-free pairings available as an option (+140 EUR).

Line-up : Alan Taudon (executive chef, formerly of the George V)

Access

Book directly with the Crillon's restaurant/concierge (rosewoodhotels.com/crillon) well in advance — only one table per service. For an August stay, aim exclusively for 27-29 August.

The turnkey stay

On the second floor of the Hôtel de Crillon, a terrace kept secret becomes a living herbarium, suspended opposite the historic salons, a few metres above the Place de la Concorde. From Thursday to Saturday, a single table for four is set there: chef Alan Taudon himself composes, in an open kitchen, a five-act menu in which the vegetal converses with the sea, at the unhurried pace of a summer evening. It is the most confidential table of the Parisian summer — four covers per evening, not one more.

Where to stay

  • La Réserve Paris — Hotel and Spa — A palace hotel in the 8th arrondissement, at the corner of Avenue Gabriel and Rue du Cirque, set back from the Champs-Élysées and a few minutes' walk from the Place de la Concorde. Awarded the Palace distinction as early as 2016, with a spa and, from its upper floors, views over the Concorde obelisk, the Grand Palais and the Eiffel Tower — the most hushed address from which to reach the Crillon on foot.
  • Le Meurice — Dorchester Collection — A historic palace hotel at 228 Rue de Rivoli, facing the Tuileries Garden, between the Place de la Concorde and the Louvre — the Crillon is a few minutes' walk away. A Parisian institution since 1835, with décor reimagined by Philippe Starck and Alain Ducasse's two-star restaurant under the same roof.

Where to dine

  • Alléno Paris au Pavillon Ledoyen — Yannick Alléno's three-Michelin-star table, in the neoclassical pavilion of the Champs-Élysées gardens, a few minutes' walk from the Place de la Concorde. Cuisine of high technical mastery, famed for its extractions and its sauces, within the most starred independent house in the world.
  • Restaurant Le Meurice Alain Ducasse — Two Michelin stars at the heart of the palace hotel Le Meurice, facing the Tuileries and a few minutes' walk from the Concorde. Executive chef Amaury Bouhours, trained at the Louis XV in Monaco, serves tasting menus that marry French tradition with contemporary creation.

What to experience

  • Visite guidée du Potager du Roi, Versailles — The perfect echo of the chef's herbarium: the kitchen garden created for Louis XIV between 1678 and 1683 by Jean-Baptiste de La Quintinie to supply the King's table, today entrusted to the École nationale supérieure de paysage. A garden listed as a historic monument, open to visitors with regular guided tours in season (times on the official website), a few minutes' walk from the Palace of Versailles.
  • Maison Guerlain, 68 avenue des Champs-Élysées — Extend the aromatic journey into an olfactory herbarium: the private mansion built by the Guerlain family in 1914, listed among the Monuments historiques and reinvented by Peter Marino, houses private salons devoted to the creation of bespoke perfume — a few minutes from the Concorde.
