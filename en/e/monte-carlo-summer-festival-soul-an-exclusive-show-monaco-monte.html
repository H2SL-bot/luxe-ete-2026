Monte-Carlo Summer Festival — SOUL! An Exclusive Show

29-30 July then from 3 to 9 August 2026 (August dates: 3 to 9 August) · Salle des Étoiles, Sporting Monte-Carlo · Festivals & Music

A new exclusive show, SOUL!, a journey through African-American music (tributes to Chuck Berry, Little Richard...), staged on 29-30 July then from 3 to 9 August; premiered at the Red Cross Gala on 18 July.

Access

Dinner-show reservations at montecarlosbm.com, from around 260 €; tables via the SBM concierge service or your hotel concierge; smart dress required, minimum age 7

The turnkey stay

An exclusive creation by the Sporting Monte-Carlo, SOUL! celebrates the flamboyant legacy of African-American music, from the rock'n'roll of Chuck Berry and Little Richard to today's sounds, in an electrifying dinner-show at the Salle des Étoiles. Preceded by the Ultramarine Girls Band performing over 150 pop, rock and Latin hits, the evening begins at 8:30pm with dinner followed by the show at 10:30pm, under a roof open to the Monegasque sky. An electric summer night in the most glamorous setting on the Riviera. Larvotto and its lights become the stage for a pure jet-set moment.

Where to stay

  • Monte-Carlo Bay Hotel & Resort — Chic resort on the Larvotto peninsula, a stone's throw from the Sporting Monte-Carlo and the Salle des Étoiles, with a lagoon, tropical gardens and the two-Michelin-star Blue Bay restaurant.
  • Hôtel de Paris Monte-Carlo — Historic palace hotel on Place du Casino, a few minutes' drive from the Sporting Monte-Carlo, a member of the Leading Hotels of the World.

Where to dine

  • Elsa — Michelin-starred restaurant at the Monte-Carlo Beach, committed Mediterranean cuisine by chef Marcel Ravin, terrace open to the sea, near Larvotto.
  • Le Louis XV - Alain Ducasse à l'Hôtel de Paris — Three-Michelin-star restaurant by Alain Ducasse, a gastronomic benchmark of the Principality since 1987, on Place du Casino.

What to experience

  • Casino de Monte-Carlo — Belle Époque emblem of Place du Casino, open to visitors in the morning for its marble atrium and historic gaming rooms, a few minutes from the Sporting.
  • Musée océanographique de Monaco — Institution founded in 1910 on the Rocher, with historic aquariums and a shark lagoon, a must-see in the Principality.
