12th Greek Maritime Golf Event

From 4 to 6 September 2026 · Costa Navarino : The Dunes Course, Navarino Dunes, Messénie · Other
✦✦✦✦ Radar score : 76/100

Twelfth edition of the golf tournament of the Greek and international maritime community, played at Costa Navarino on The Dunes Course, the 18-hole signature course of the Messenian estate. For three days, shipowning executives, insurers, registries and shipyards meet on the links for a gathering that is as much a tournament as an informal congress of Greek shipping. Each year the tournament pairs a charitable initiative with its closing ceremony.

Line-up : Greek and international shipping executives. Maritime sponsors announced on the official website: IRI / The Marshall Islands Registry, Mainline Shipping Company, DNV, Arrow, Standard Club; partners Under Armour, Sixt and Marine Tours.

How to get in

Greek Maritime Golf Event. The official website names no organising company and no individual organiser.

Go through the contact form on the official website (no email or telephone is published), then book accommodation with Costa Navarino.

The turnkey stay

Three days in September when the elite of Greek and international shipping leaves the boardrooms for the fairways of the Dunes Course. Greece's first signature course, designed by Bernhard Langer with European Golf Design and open since 2010, this par 71 of 6,581 yards unfolds right beside The Romanos and the Westin: links facing the Ionian Sea near the shore, then olive groves and citrus orchards as you climb inland along the Sellas river. Below opens the Bay of Navarino, one of the largest natural harbours in the Mediterranean, where the fleets sunk in the 1827 battle still rest: the most fitting setting there is for a tournament born of the maritime world. Deals are struck on the green as much as at the table, between Navarino Dunes beach and the terraces opening onto the bay.

Where to stay

  • The Romanos, a Luxury Collection Resort, Costa Navarino — On the Navarino Dunes estate itself (official address: Navarino Dunes, Messinia, 24001), that is, the site that hosts the Dunes Course: the tournament venue can be reached without taking the car. Rooms from 43 sq m with private infinity pool and uninterrupted views over the Ionian Sea, seafront suites and villas with butler service. Access to the Anazoe Spa, a 4,000 sq m spa and thalassotherapy centre on the same estate, and to some ten dining venues including Barbouni, on the beach. Ranked No. 1 among Greek resorts at the Condé Nast Traveler Readers' Choice Awards 2024 (US edition).
  • Mandarin Oriental, Costa Navarino — The first Mandarin Oriental in Greece, set at Navarino Bay — the second zone of Costa Navarino, a few minutes' drive from Navarino Dunes — facing the historic Bay of Navarino. Suites with bay views and semi-buried villas with private pool, designed for privacy. Waterside wellness area: 25 m pool, olive-wood sauna, plunge pool, hammam and steam rooms. The resort and its spa are rated five stars by the Forbes Travel Guide, and the spa was named World's Best Resort Spa 2025 at the World Spa Awards. Eight dining outlets, from contemporary Greek (Oliviera) to French brasserie.

Where to dine

  • Barbouni Restaurant — The beachside institution of Navarino Dunes, on the very estate hosting the tournament, attached to The Romanos. Mediterranean seafood cuisine: freshly caught fish and house specialities, feet in the sand facing the Ionian Sea. Note that this is a lunch venue: open daily from 11:00 to 17:30 only — to slot in between two rounds, not for dinner.
  • The Private Kitchen, Mandarin Oriental, Costa Navarino — The chef's table of the Mandarin Oriental, at Navarino Bay: a counter facing the open kitchen, where chef Bertrand Valegeas and his brigade run the service before your eyes. A bespoke menu built on exceptional local produce from the gardens and farms of Messenia. Dinner only on Saturday and Sunday, from 19:30 — to be booked well in advance, as the number of covers is confidential.

What to experience

  • Anazoe Spa, spa et thalassothérapie de Navarino Dunes — 4,000 sq m of spa and thalassotherapy centre at the heart of Navarino Dunes, on the very estate of the tournament. 21 treatment rooms, a Heat & Ice zone, hydrotherapy pools opening onto the herb and olive garden, hydromassage baths and ice baths. The signature treatments draw on the health and beauty practices of ancient Greece and on the resources of Messenia — olive oil, herbs, minerals — across five strands including The Messinian Healing and The Kinetics Flow, the latter tailored to muscle recovery. More than twenty international distinctions. The exact counterpoint to three days on the course.
  • Navarino Yachting, affrètement privé dans la baie de Navarino — The natural extension of a maritime tournament: a private cruise departing from Pylos, in the historic waters of the Bay of Navarino, scene of the 1827 battle. A company based in Pylos and active since 2012, offering half-day or full-day outings. A fleet of seven vessels: motor yachts M/Y Nestor, Nilefs and Sorokos, sailing yacht S/Y Mykines, catamaran S/Y Aria and two speedboats. Tailor-made itineraries to Sfaktiria, Voidokilia, Methoni, Sapientza, Foinikounda or the island of Proti. Skipper, fuel, VAT, wines and a snack included; several boats may be chartered for a group. The company also trades as Navarino Sea Yachting.
