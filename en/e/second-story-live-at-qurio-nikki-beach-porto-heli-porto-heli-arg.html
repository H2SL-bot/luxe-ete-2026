Second Story Live at Qurio – Nikki Beach Porto Heli

August 15, 2026, from 8 pm to 11 pm · Qurio Rooftop Bar, Nikki Beach Resort & Spa Porto Heli, Argolide · Polo · Motoring · Sailing

On August 15, the absolute peak of the Greek season, the Qurio rooftop at Nikki Beach Porto Heli hosts the Athenian DJ duo Second Story, billed by the house as world rhythms and immersive sounds beneath the August sky. The resort is the Greek anchor of the Saint-Tropez and Saint-Barth beach-club brand, set in the most sheltered bay of the Peloponnese, facing the yachts at anchor off Porto Heli.

Line-up : Live set by the Athenian duo Second Story; a crowd of yachtsmen, Porto Heli villa owners and Nikki Beach regulars.

How to get in

Nikki Beach Resort & Spa Porto Heli (Nikki Beach Hotels & Resorts).

Book a table at Qurio through the resort (phone or email), or a room for the night of August 15.

The turnkey stay

On August 15, the day of the greatest feast of the Greek summer, Second Story takes to the roof of the Nikki Beach Resort & Spa Porto Heli. Qurio occupies the hotel's top terrace and opens from 8 pm to 11 pm: signature cocktails, selected wines and exceptional spirits, while the golds of sunset spread across the water and the village of Porto Heli lights up at your feet. Across the water lies Spetses, the island where cars remain off-limits to visitors, a few minutes away by water taxi; behind, the Argolid of the ancient theatres. This tip of the Peloponnese has become the Greek Riviera without ever taking on its noise.

Where to stay

  • Nikki Beach Resort & Spa Porto Heli — You sleep above the party: Qurio is this hotel's rooftop terrace, open Tuesday to Sunday from 8 pm to 11 pm. 66 rooms and suites, plus 6 villas and bungalows. The resort's beach and pool, the Idolo Spa (treatments blending the wisdom of ancient Greek massage with Greek products), and the Nikki Beach Restaurant for Greek and international cuisine, from breakfast to dinner. Address: Argolida, Peloponnese 21061, Greece.
  • Amanzoe — The region's Aman palace, at Agios Panteleimonas, Kranidi (Argolida), on the heights of the Peloponnese's east coast near Porto Heli. Three families of accommodation: hillside pavilions with sea and countryside views, Beach Pavilions by the water, and villas among the olive trees with private pools. A flagship Aman Spa using natural products, a private beach club, and five dining venues including Akari, the Japanese table. Allow 2.5 hours by road from Athens airport, or 25 minutes by helicopter.

Where to dine

  • Patralis Fish Tavern — The institution of Spetses, founded in 1935 by "Uncle Panos", a fisherman of the island, and still family-run: his son Vasilis and his grandsons Argyris and Nikos. Fresh fish of the day, lobster and slipper lobster, fried calamari, moussaka, saganaki. In Kounoupitsa, open from noon to midnight all year except November and December; booking strongly advised in high summer. Reached from the Kosta pier, next to Porto Heli, in a few minutes by water taxi.
  • Orloff Restaurant — An emblematic table of the Old Harbour of Spetses, established since 1991 in the neoclassical building that housed the island's first harbourmaster's office in 1802. Greek flavours served under the stars on what the house claims is the most beautiful terrace in Spetses. Reservations at +30 22980 75255. Same water-taxi crossing from Kosta.

What to experience

  • Transfert hélicoptère privé Athènes, Porto Heli / Amanzoe (HELISTAR) — Arrive by air rather than by road: HELISTAR S.A., a Greek operator based in Koropi (Pithagora 50 heliport), links Athens with Amanzoe / Kranidi, near Porto Heli, in around 22 minutes of flight. Airbus fleet: AS365 Dauphin, H135, H120. Operations seven days a week from 7 am to 9 pm, on demand.
  • Une tragédie antique au Théâtre antique d'Épidaure (Athens Epidaurus Festival) — The great summer event of the Argolid, inland from Porto Heli: the Athens Epidaurus Festival, founded in 1955, stages ancient drama at the Ancient Theatre of Epidaurus — in 2026, The Trojan Women, Antigone, Lysistrata, Ion. The season runs until August 31; check exact dates on the official programme, as changes and cancellations are announced over the summer. Entry to the theatre is forbidden once the performance has begun, except at the interval: so you arrive early — which, at Epidaurus, is the best part of the programme.
