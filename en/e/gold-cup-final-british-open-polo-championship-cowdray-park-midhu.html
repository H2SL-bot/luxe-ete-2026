Gold Cup Final — British Open Polo Championship (Cowdray Park)

Final on 19 July 2026 (tournament from 24 June to 19 July) · Cowdray Park Polo Club, The Lawns · Polo · Motoring · Sailing

The final of Europe's most prestigious polo tournament (22-goal High Goal level), 'the home of British polo'. A highly social day: luxury hospitality, champagne, the divot-stomping tradition, grandstands and private boxes. NB: the Cartier Queen's Cup at Guards Polo Club is played in mid-June (outside the July–August window).

Access

Tickets and hospitality via cowdraypolo.co.uk; VIP packages (Grandstand, Seriously Cool Events)

The turnkey stay

The great final of English polo at Cowdray, champagne and divot-stomping, prolonged in a manor beloved of international players, at the heart of the estate.

Where to stay

  • Park House Hotel & Spa — A luxury family manor with a mother-of-pearl pool in its spa, 10 minutes from the grounds — historic haunt of the polo fraternity.
  • The Spread Eagle Hotel & Spa — A coaching inn dating from 1430 in the heart of Midhurst, with a contemporary spa, minutes from Cowdray Park.

Where to dine

  • Restaurant Interlude — One Michelin star (7th year) plus a Green Star, chef Jean Delport, tasting menu in the gardens of Leonardslee.
  • The Spread Eagle Restaurant — Award-winning modern British cooking in the setting of a 1430 inn, a stone's throw from the polo estate.

What to experience

  • Cowdray Park Golf Club — An 18-hole championship course since 1904, on the polo estate itself, facing the South Downs.
  • Park House Spa — An intimate spa with a 15 m mother-of-pearl-lined indoor pool, sauna and hammam set within gardens.
