London Fashion Week — Spring/Summer 2027

17-21 September 2026 · London (various venues) · Divers lieux (British Fashion Council) · Fashion & Couture
✓ Verified at the source on 15/07/2026

British boldness and it-girls: London electrifies the season with young designers and a royal front row. The most creative stop of the Big Four, a showcase for emerging talent.

Line-up : British designers, it-girls, fashion press

How to get in

British Fashion Council (BFC), official organiser of London Fashion Week; the Spring-Summer 2027 show calendar takes place in London from Thursday 17 to Monday 21 September 2026 (dates confirmed by the BFC).

No public ticketing for the shows. The BFC does NOT issue invitations: front-row seats remain at the discretion of the brands and their PR agencies. Realistic avenues: (1) professional press accreditation via the BFC portal, reserved for accredited journalists/photographers/buyers with proof of commission and recent publications; (2) being invited directly by a house or its press officer; (3) certain open experiences (DiscoveryLAB, Community events) accessible upon registration. Accreditation should be requested a few weeks before the event.

The turnkey stay

From 17 to 21 September 2026 — dates confirmed by the British Fashion Council — London unveils the silhouettes of spring-summer 2027, with the shows spread across various venues in the heart of the capital. Setting down your bags on the Aldwych–Strand axis, between Covent Garden and the Thames, places you as close as can be to the show circuit, between two glasses of champagne and dinner in Covent Garden. Five days in which the British capital marries the daring of its young designers with the composure of its century-old institutions.

Where to stay

  • The Savoy — The icon of the Strand, standing on the Thames since 1889, at the heart of the show perimeter between Covent Garden and Aldwych. Suites overlooking the river, tea beneath the glass roof of The Gallery — a ritual upheld since 1889 — and the address where London has been celebrating its great evenings for more than 130 years.
  • One Aldwych — A discreet five-star set on the Aldwych crescent, steps from the Strand and Covent Garden — ideally placed for moving between shows. Rooms and suites of a so British elegance, an 18-metre chlorine-free pool, a spa with sauna, and the Indigo restaurant to catch your breath between two shows.

Where to dine

  • Frog by Adam Handling — One Michelin star on Southampton Street, in the heart of Covent Garden, facing The Savoy. Since 2017 Scottish chef Adam Handling MBE has been crafting a sustainable British luxury here, inventive and theatrical — a few minutes' walk from the show circuit.
  • Rules — London's oldest restaurant, founded in 1798 on Maiden Lane, in Covent Garden. Classic game, wood panelling and two centuries of writers, actors and crowned heads: the absolute institution, a few minutes' walk from the Strand.

What to experience

  • Arrivée héliportée au London Heliport — Reach London by air via the London Heliport in Battersea, the capital's only CAA-licensed heliport, on the south bank of the Thames. Airport transfers and private charters, to arrive at the shows the way one steps on stage.
  • Sur-mesure chez Huntsman, Savile Row — Step through the door of 11 Savile Row, a tailoring house founded in 1849, for a bespoke commission: fittings, a library of cloths and the house tweed experience. The quintessence of British elegance, in perfect echo of Fashion Week.
