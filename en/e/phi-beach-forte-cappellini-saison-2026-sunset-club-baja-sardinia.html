Phi Beach (Forte Cappellini) — 2026 Sunset & Club Season

Summer season, July–August 2026 · Phi Beach, Forte Cappellini, Baja Sardinia (vue coucher de soleil sur la baie) · Festivals & Music

VERIFIED (2+ sources: DJ Mag, Loudlife, Mixmag). The most scenographic open-air club on the Costa (a cult sunset). A packed 2026 programme; Peggy Gou makes her debut, Black Coffee returns. Line-up announced progressively through the season.

Line-up : Black Coffee, Peggy Gou, Fisher, Eric Prydz, Meduza, Loco Dice, The Martinez Brothers, Jamie Jones, Bob Sinclar, Claptone, Honey Dijon, Adriatique, Damian Lazarus, Seth Troxler, Kungs, James Hype

Access

Tickets via tickets.phibeach.com; sunbed / club table / restaurant (Luciano's) bookings. Book ahead for sunsets and headline DJs.

The turnkey stay

Dance at sunset on the Forte Cappellini, then let the Costa Smeralda hold you: a palace facing the bay, a starred table and an emerald sea.

Where to stay

  • 7Pines Resort Sardinia — A seafront five-star at Baja Sardinia, minutes from Phi Beach, with a signature spa and a Michelin star on site.
  • Hotel Cala di Volpe, a Luxury Collection Hotel — The mythical Couelle-designed palace hotel at Porto Cervo, the glamour address of the Costa Smeralda, around 20 minutes from the club.

Where to dine

  • Capogiro — One Michelin star, chef Pasquale D'Ambrosio, panoramic terrace over the bay, at the 7Pines just steps from the club.
  • ConFusion — One Michelin star from chef Italo Bassi, Porto Cervo's only starred table, Mediterranean auteur cuisine.

What to experience

  • Charter yacht prive vers La Maddalena — Private excursion from Porto Cervo to the La Maddalena archipelago, turquoise waters and secret coves.
  • Pure Seven Spa au 7Pines — A signature spa set around a leafy patio: hammam, couples' treatments and holistic wellness rituals.
