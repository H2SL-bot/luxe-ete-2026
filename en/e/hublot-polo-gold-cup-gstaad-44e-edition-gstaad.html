Hublot Polo Gold Cup Gstaad — 44th edition

From 20 to 23 August 2026 · Polo Club Gstaad (Saanen) · Polo Club Gstaad, terrain de l'aérodrome de Saanen · Polo · Motoring · Sailing

The most fashionable polo gathering in the Alps: at 1,000 m altitude, the 44th Hublot Polo Gold Cup brings together the German-Swiss elite, the grand hotels of Gstaad (Gstaad Palace, The Alpina) and the watchmaking sponsors around the Saanen field. Private jets, champagne and chukkas in the Bernese Oberland.

Line-up : German-Swiss elite, the Gstaad chalet set, sponsor Hublot

How to get in

Polo Club Gstaad, organiser of the Hublot Polo Gold Cup Gstaad (44th edition)

Two options: watch the matches free of charge at the edge of the field, or book a table / a VIP hospitality place (Chukker Club, partner tents) with event manager Solina Mela Mobèche (info@pologstaad.ch, +41 33 744 07 40) or at polo-gstaad.ch/reservation. Book early: the Gstaad chalet set fills the tents quickly.

The turnkey stay

Every summer, the Polo Gold Cup turns the Gstaad valley into a playground for the international elite, hooves in the grass and champagne flutes beneath the Bernese Alps. A stay at the Gstaad Palace is a must — its white silhouette has crowned the village since 1913 — or at the neighbouring The Alpina for a starred table by Martin Göschel. The evening ends, as it should, at the legendary GreenGo, the Palace's nightclub, unchanged since the seventies.

Where to stay

  • Gstaad Palace — Historic 1913 palace hotel overlooking the village of Gstaad, a member of the Leading Hotels of the World, the benchmark for alpine high society for over a century.
  • The Alpina Gstaad — Contemporary 5-star hotel opened in 2012, wood and stone chalets, recognised by the Michelin Guide for its gastronomic restaurant.

Where to dine

  • Restaurant Sommet — Michelin-starred restaurant at The Alpina Gstaad, cuisine by chef Martin Göschel blending French classics with Saanenland produce, a cellar of over 400 labels.
  • Le Grand Restaurant & La Terrasse — Gastronomic restaurant at the Gstaad Palace, fully renovated in 2025, tableside service and views of the alpine peaks.

What to experience

  • GreenGo Club — Legendary nightclub at the Gstaad Palace, with its original 1970s disco décor, the nocturnal meeting point of the alpine jet-set.
  • Golfclub Gstaad-Saanenland — 18-hole golf course founded in 1959 in Saanenmöser, nestled within a nationally protected wetland, surrounded by mountain peaks.
