Artcurial — sale "La Modernité en partage" (Louis Grandchamp des Raux Collection, in the orbit of Art Basel Paris)

Saturday, October 24, 2026, during Art Basel Paris week · Artcurial, Hôtel Marcel Dassault, 7 Rond-Point des Champs-Élysées, 75008 Paris · Art & Auctions

A landmark private-collection sale held in Artcurial's historic mansion on the Champs-Élysées, with a preview and cocktail reception ahead of the auction — a classic rendezvous for collectors and dealers during Art Basel Paris week.

How to get in

Artcurial, auction house housed in the Hôtel Marcel Dassault, 7 Rond-Point des Champs-Élysées

Free public preview and exhibition before the sale. To bid, register via artcurial.com; for any professional inquiry, go through the general contact, who will direct you to the right department.

The turnkey stay

A few minutes' walk from the Hôtel Marcel Dassault, the George V closes out the evening: after the auction, head to Le Cinq for a three-star dinner in the heart of the Golden Triangle.

Where to stay

  • Four Seasons Hôtel George V — A palace hotel in the Golden Triangle, a few minutes' walk from Artcurial, famed for its floral displays and its Guerlain spa.

Where to dine

  • Le Cinq (George V) — 3 Michelin stars, chef Christian Le Squer, Régence dining room at the George V.
