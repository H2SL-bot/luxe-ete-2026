Watches and Wonders Geneva 2027

From Monday 5 to Sunday 11 April 2027 at Palexpo. New this edition: the public weekend extends to three days, from Friday 9 to Sunday 11 April. · Palexpo, route François-Peyrot 30, 1218 Le Grand-Saconnex (Genève) · Jewellery & Watchmaking
✦✦✦ Radar score : 69/100
✓ Verified at the source on 24/08/2026

The great worldwide gathering of fine watchmaking: the maisons unveil their new releases for the year at Palexpo, before nearly 60,000 visitors. The 2027 edition expands: a full week, a public weekend extended to three days, and the arrival of Breitling, Universal Genève, Gallet and the jeweller Damiani among the exhibitors.

How to get in

A strictly trade-only fair from 5 to 8 April 2027 (accredited attendees only: press, retail, houses) — badges collected at the accreditation desk, Entrance 1 of Palexpo. Press access: invitation from an exhibiting house or direct contact with the official press office (press@watchesandwonders.org); there is no open accreditation desk. Open to the general public from 9 to 11 April 2027, with online ticketing at watchesandwonders.com. Paid VIP option: the Premium Package (dedicated badge access, private entrance, lounge, expert-guided tour).

The fair is not a standard public ticket event: 5-8 April is reserved for accredited professionals/press only. The general public is admitted only on 9-11 April 2027, tickets sold online at watchesandwonders.com (2027 rates not yet published). For reference, the 2026 edition charged around CHF 50-70 per day, with a 3-day pass at CHF 160, plus reduced student/senior rates. For easier access on the watchmaking-house side, the official Premium Package (CHF 550/person in 2026, ages 12+) includes private entry, a VIP lounge with meals and an expert-guided tour — 2027 terms and pricing to be confirmed on the official website.

The turnkey stay

For the world's most sought-after watchmaking week, Geneva unfurls its palace hotels along the shore of Lake Geneva: suites with views of Mont Blanc, starred restaurants where new releases are discussed in intimate settings, and a lake crossed by Belle Époque steamers between manufacture appointments. A jet-set interlude between watchmaking business and the Swiss art of living, a stone's throw from Palexpo yet a world away from its bustle.

Where to stay

  • Hôtel Beau-Rivage Genève — A family-run palace hotel dating from 1865 on Quai du Mont-Blanc, a historic haunt of high society and watchmaking houses during fair week. A general renovation of the hotel has been announced through the end of February 2027 (schedule subject to change): reconfirm before booking for fair week.
  • Hotel President Wilson, a Luxury Collection Hotel — A 5-star address on Quai Wilson, with views of the lake and the Alps, a spa and signature suites — one of the usual VIP bases for watchmaking week.

Where to dine

  • Le Chat-Botté (Hôtel Beau-Rivage) — Michelin-starred since 2010, refined French cuisine by Mathieu Croze; the establishment is under renovation with reopening announced for early 2027, to be reconfirmed before booking.
  • Bayview (Hotel President Wilson) — One Michelin star, panoramic lake views and a table suspended beneath Saint-Louis crystal; French cuisine created by Michel Roth, now led by chef Danny Khezzar.

What to experience

  • Croisière Belle Époque CGN sur le Léman — Board the Compagnie Générale de Navigation's historic paddle steamers, cruising between Geneva, Lausanne and the French shores of the lake.
  • Musée Patek Philippe — Five centuries of watchmaking and automata in the setting of the Plainpalais district — the ideal heritage counterpoint to the fair's frenzy of new releases.
