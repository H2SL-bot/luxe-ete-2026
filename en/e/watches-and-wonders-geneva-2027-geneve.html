Watches and Wonders Geneva 2027

From Monday 5 to Sunday 11 April 2027 at Palexpo. New this edition: the public weekend extends to three days, from Friday 9 to Sunday 11 April. · Palexpo, route François-Peyrot 30, 1218 Le Grand-Saconnex (Genève) · Jewellery & Watchmaking
✓ Verified at the source on 24/08/2026

The great worldwide gathering of fine watchmaking: the maisons unveil their new releases for the year at Palexpo, before nearly 60,000 visitors. The 2027 edition expands: a full week, a public weekend extended to three days, and the arrival of Breitling, Universal Genève, Gallet and the jeweller Damiani among the exhibitors.

Access

Trade days (press, retailers, guests of the maisons) from Monday 5 April; open to the public from Friday 9 to Sunday 11 April, with tickets. 2027 prices not yet published; for reference, the Palexpo page lists 70 CHF per day (40 CHF for students). Accreditation via the Watches and Wonders Geneva Foundation.
