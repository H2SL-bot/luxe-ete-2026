Salone del Mobile Milano 2027, 65th edition

From 13 to 18 April 2027 at Rho Fiera. General public on the weekend of 17 and 18 April only; trade visitors all week. · Rho Fiera Milano, Strada Statale del Sempione 28, 20017 Rho (Milan) · Palace Hotels & Fine Dining
✦✦✦ Radar score : 64/100
✓ Verified at the source on 24/08/2026

The 65th edition of the world's largest design fair, with the return of Euroluce, the lighting biennial, an inaugural Salone Contract Forum whose layout is designed by Rem Koolhaas and OMA, and the SaloneSatellite for young designers. More than 316,000 visitors in 2026; the final weekend opens the aisles of Rho to the general public.

How to get in

Trade accreditation: online registration at salonemilano.it, reserved for architects, designers, distributors and importers — badge valid from 13 to 18 April 2027 (suppliers/manufacturers: 16-18 April). Press accreditation: application via salonemilano.it/en/press, reviewed individually (valid press card, an article <12 months old, or a letter from the editorial office). Questions: tickets@salonemilano.it.

Public days: Saturday 17 and Sunday 18 April 2027, visitor hours 9:30am-6:30pm (press 8:30am-6:30pm). 2027 rates not yet published; in 2026, general-public tickets ranged from €38 to €53 depending on purchase timing (online presale cheaper than at the door). Purchase/information: salonemilano.it/en/admissions-and-tickets, enquiries: tickets@salonemilano.it.

The turnkey stay

For the Salone del Mobile, the jet set forgoes the fairground hotels to settle into the Quadrilatero della Moda and Brera, between suites signed Bulgari or Mandarin Oriental, starred dinners and rooftops overlooking the rooftops of Milan. The ritual: trade accreditation collected in the morning at Rho Fiera, then afternoons devoted to the historic showrooms of Via Durini and the Fuorisalone, before the city shifts into evening mode — cocktails on terraces, gastronomic tables booked months in advance, and a last drink beside a pool suspended above Milan.

Where to stay

  • Bvlgari Hotel Milano — An 18th-century palazzo between Montenapoleone, Via della Spiga and La Scala, with a private garden and Bulgari spa; the absolute reference for a jet-set stay during the Salone.
  • Mandarin Oriental, Milan — A historic palazzo near La Scala and the Duomo, 104 rooms and suites, with an on-site spa and the two-starred Seta restaurant.
  • Portrait Milano — A former archiepiscopal seminary converted into a hotel-residence by the Lungarno Collection (Ferragamo), in the heart of the fashion district, a member of Leading Hotels of the World.

Where to dine

  • Seta by Antonio Guida (Mandarin Oriental, Milan) — Two Michelin stars, contemporary Italian cuisine by chef Antonio Guida; one of Milan's most sought-after tables during the Salone.
  • Cracco in Galleria — Carlo Cracco's starred restaurant in the heart of the Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II, overlooking the Ottagono, an iconic address in the historic centre.

What to experience

  • Ceresio7 Pools & Restaurant — A rooftop by Dsquared2 (Dean and Dan Caten), with two pools suspended above the rooftops of Milan, a must for design week evenings.
  • Showroom historique Cassina, Via Durini — A 2,000 sqm flagship of manufacturer Cassina, opened in 1968 on Via Durini in the heart of Milan's historic design district; private visits/appointments on request.
