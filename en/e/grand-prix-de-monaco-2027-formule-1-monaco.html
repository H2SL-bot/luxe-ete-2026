Monaco Grand Prix 2027, Formula 1

From Thursday 3 to Sunday 6 June 2027, with the race on Sunday. Dates published by the Automobile Club de Monaco, still subject to validation by the FIA World Motor Sport Council: the 2027 F1 calendar will be approved in autumn 2026. · Circuit urbain de Monte-Carlo, Principauté de Monaco ; organisation Automobile Club de Monaco, 44 rue Grimaldi · Polo · Motoring · Sailing
✦✦✦✦ Radar score : 86/100

The 84th edition of the most legendary race on the calendar, first round of the European leg: Formula 1 in the streets of the Principality, between the port, the casino and the tunnel, beneath the most coveted terraces in world sport. The first full weekend of June is now Monaco's fixed slot on the calendar, under an agreement running until 2031.

How to get in

Official ticketing at monaco-grandprix.com or at the ACM ticket office (44 rue Grimaldi, Monaco; tel. 00 377 93 15 26 24, Mon-Fri 9am-6pm); 2027 sales not yet open. VIP Belvédère/Loges packages: hospitalite@acm.mc. Press: ACM form (national), FIA portal (international); TV/radio/photographers via Formula One Management's media accreditation service (formula1.com).

ACM categories: GOLD grandstands (sectors K1/K2/K3/K6, L, B, V) and SILVER, VIP BELVEDERE and LOGES packages (lounge, catering included); -10% on 2- or 3-day packages. The F1 Paddock Club is distributed via formula1.com. 2027 rates not yet published to date: refer to the 2026 pricing grid for reference until ticketing opens at monaco-grandprix.com.

The turnkey stay

The Monaco Grand Prix is not just a race: it is THE jet-set gathering of the Riviera, where an urban circuit, yachts moored in the Darse Nord and Belle Époque palace hotels form a single open-air stage. Settling in at the Fairmont facing the legendary hairpin, at the Hôtel de Paris or the Métropole, dining at Alain Ducasse's Louis XV, then extending the night beneath the chandeliers of the Casino de Monte-Carlo: this is the ultimate Monegasque experience, to be booked months in advance as the best views over the circuit are snapped up every spring.

Where to stay

  • Hôtel de Paris Monte-Carlo — The historic palace hotel on Place du Casino, the beating heart of the Grand Prix, a stone's throw from the paddocks and the Rock.
  • Hôtel Métropole Monte-Carlo — A Belle Époque palace hotel designed by Jacques Garcia in the Carré d'Or, a few metres from the Casino and the circuit.
  • Fairmont Monte Carlo — The hotel built around the legendary Fairmont Hairpin, Formula 1's slowest corner: suites and terraces with a bird's-eye view over the circuit.

Where to dine

  • Le Louis XV - Alain Ducasse à l'Hôtel de Paris — Alain Ducasse's three-Michelin-star restaurant, the absolute reference of Monegasque gastronomy since 1987.

What to experience

  • Casino de Monte-Carlo — The legendary Belle Époque casino, gaming salons and the emblematic façade of Place du Casino, the epicentre of the Grand Prix.
  • Virage du Fairmont Hairpin — The circuit's most iconic viewpoint: the championship's slowest hairpin, to be observed from the balconies and terraces overlooking the track.
