Monaco Grand Prix 2027, Formula 1

From Thursday 3 to Sunday 6 June 2027, with the race on Sunday. Dates published by the Automobile Club de Monaco, still subject to validation by the FIA World Motor Sport Council: the 2027 F1 calendar will be approved in autumn 2026. · Circuit urbain de Monte-Carlo, Principauté de Monaco ; organisation Automobile Club de Monaco, 44 rue Grimaldi · Polo · Motoring · Sailing

The 84th edition of the most legendary race on the calendar, first round of the European leg: Formula 1 in the streets of the Principality, between the port, the casino and the tunnel, beneath the most coveted terraces in world sport. The first full weekend of June is now Monaco's fixed slot on the calendar, under an agreement running until 2031.

Access

A single official ticketing outlet: monaco-grandprix.com (ACM). The 2027 sale opens 'in the coming weeks'; sign up to the ACM newsletter to be notified, as the best grandstands and terraces go as soon as sales open. Published structure: Thursday at a reduced rate (free for 6-15 year olds), 2-day (Sat-Sun) and 3-day (Fri-Sun) packages with a 10% discount on seated places, children under 6 free on laps.
