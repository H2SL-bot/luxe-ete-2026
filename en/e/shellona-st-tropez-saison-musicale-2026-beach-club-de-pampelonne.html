Shellona St-Tropez — 2026 music season (Pampelonne beach club)

Summer 2026 season (late May–late September), DJ programming according to the club calendar. PLEASE NOTE: the dated headline nights (Black Coffee 16 July & 4 Aug., Solomun 18 July, Rüfüs Du Sol 26 July, Peggy Gou 28 July, Diplo 12 Aug., Damian Lazarus 17 Aug.) are announced by concierge sources (AC Private) and NOT confirmed by the official Shellona website — dates to be verified with the club. · Shellona, plage de Pampelonne, Chemin des Tamaris · Festivals & Music

A Greek-chic beach club on Pampelonne that turns into an electronic stage from the afternoon onwards. According to concierges (AC Private), the 2026 season should line up a roster of world-class DJs (Solomun, Black Coffee, Peggy Gou, Rüfüs Du Sol, Diplo...), yet the official website publishes no such dated line-up. Champagne-lunch mood, then dancing at sunset. Access: lunch with no minimum spend (150-300 € per person) or a table with minimum spend on event nights.

Line-up : Solomun, Black Coffee (Grammy), Peggy Gou, Rüfüs Du Sol, Diplo, Damian Lazarus, Ben Böhmer, Jamie Jones, Joseph Capriati, Adriatique

How to get in

Shellona (Shellona group)

Table reservation via SevenRooms (official link from shellona.com), or official phone/email

The turnkey stay

Three suspended days on the peninsula: umbrella pines, terraced vines and that late-afternoon light that tips Pampelonne from idleness into celebration. Base yourself in Ramatuelle or on the heights of Saint-Tropez, days spent between golden sand, a cru classé cellar and a three-star table, evening at Shellona, chemin des Tamaris, when the decks take over from the sun. Concierge note: the 2026 DJ programming varies with the club's calendar, and the announced headline nights (Black Coffee, Solomun, Rüfüs Du Sol, Peggy Gou, Diplo, Damian Lazarus) come from third-party sources not confirmed by Shellona — we have the date and the table confirmed directly with the club before any booking.

Where to stay

  • La Réserve Ramatuelle, Hôtel, Spa & Villas — A confidential palace hotel set in the garrigue above the bay of Pampelonne: 12 villas and suites designed by Rémi Tessier, Nescens spa with indoor pool and hammam, Michelin-starred restaurant La Voile by chef Éric Canino. A few minutes from the beaches and from the village of Saint-Tropez — the most discreet address for coming home late without meeting a soul.
  • Airelles Saint-Tropez, Château de la Messardière — A 19th-century château with turrets and arcades, 12 hectares of cypress and jasmine overlooking the beaches of Pampelonne and the vineyards of Ramatuelle. 86 rooms and suites, ten restaurants and bars, a 1,000 m² Valmont spa with adults-only indoor pool and nine treatment rooms.

Where to dine

  • La Vague d'Or, Cheval Blanc St-Tropez — Three Michelin stars, 19/20 in the Gault&Millau. Arnaud Donckele signs a cuisine built on the fish of the Mediterranean, his jus, sauces and vinaigrettes now signatures. Terrace facing the baie des Canoubiers, beneath the pines — the gala dinner of the stay, to be booked far in advance.
  • Chez Camille, Bonne Terrasse, Ramatuelle — A family house born in 1912 as a fisherman's hut, a restaurant since 1953, in the secret cove of Bonne Terrasse. Wood-fired bouillabaisse (scorpionfish, gurnard, John Dory from the gulf), spiny lobster and the day's catch, feet in the sand. Boat shuttle available: the peninsula's authentic counterpoint.

What to experience

  • Le Club 55, plage de Pampelonne, Ramatuelle — The Pampelonne institution since 1955, 43 boulevard Patch, a few hundred metres from Shellona. A lunch of wild fish and crudités under the tamarisks, mattresses, parasols and straw huts, pontoon and boat shuttle. Open daily from 10:00 to 17:00 — the ideal morning before a night at the beach club.
  • Château Minuty, cru classé, Gassin — A 170-hectare family estate perched on the hills of Gassin, one of the Côtes de Provence crus classés since 1955 and one of the last still harvesting by hand. Guided tour of the estate and the cellar followed by a tasting of three wines, with a panoramic view over the sea and the hilltop villages.
