The Aspen Art Fair 2026

from Wednesday, July 29 to Saturday, August 1, 2026 · Hotel Jerome, Aspen, Colorado · Art & Auctions

Third edition of the fair taking over the Hotel Jerome: more than thirty-five international exhibitors install their works throughout the palace hotel, right into the guest rooms. The program announces talks, performances, screenings, prizes, artist residencies, private collection visits and site-specific installations. The fair is led by Kelly Cornell, alongside co-founder Bob Chase, owner of the Hexton gallery in Aspen.

Line-up : Bob Chase, co-founder of the fair and owner of the Hexton gallery; Kelly Cornell, director of the 2026 edition.

How to get in

The Aspen Art Fair — directed by Kelly Cornell, co-founded by Bob Chase (Hexton Gallery)

Take the most complete pass offered on aspenartfair.org: it is the tier that opens the previews and the parallel program (private collection visits). Check the exact offer on the site's ticketing page.
