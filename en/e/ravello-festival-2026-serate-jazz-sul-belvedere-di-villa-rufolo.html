Ravello Festival 2026 — Jazz Nights on the Belvedere of Villa Rufolo

6, 7 and 8 August 2026 (three jazz nights), 21:30 · Belvedere di Villa Rufolo, Ravello · Festivals & Music

On the legendary Belvedere suspended above the Amalfi Coast, three jazz nights from the 74th edition: Andrea Motis Trio (6th), Rita Marcotulli Ensemble (7th) and “Omaggio a Miles” with saxophonist Rick Margitza — who played alongside Miles Davis — Jeff Ballard, Dario Deidda… (8th). A glamorous bucket-list setting, sunset over infinity. Real access: official ticketing (from 40 €) online and box office on Piazza Duomo. (NB: other concerts from the same edition are already on the radar; these are the jazz nights, a separate strand.)

Line-up : Rick Margitza (formerly with Miles Davis), Jeff Ballard, Andrea Motis, Rita Marcotulli, Dario Deidda

How to get in

Ticketing open

Purchase online on the official Fondazione Ravello website (fondazioneravello.com) or at the box office on Piazza Duomo, Ravello

The turnkey stay

On 6 August 2026 at 9:30pm, the Belvedere of Villa Rufolo swaps the grand symphonic repertoire for the mellow swing of the Ravello Festival's Serate Jazz, opening with the Andrea Motis Trio, followed on subsequent evenings by the Rita Marcotulli Ensemble and a tribute to Miles Davis. Three nights of jazz facing the sea, in the same setting that hosts Wagner and Rattle a few weeks later. A more intimate interlude, just as breathtaking in its views, closing out the summer gently on the cliffs of Ravello.

Where to stay

  • Caruso, A Belmond Hotel, Amalfi Coast — 11th-century palace perched 350 m above the sea, with an emblematic infinity pool and terraced gardens, a short distance from Villa Rufolo.
  • Palazzo Avino — 5-star hotel housed in a 12th-century palace in the centre of Ravello, with panoramic terraces and a discreet jet-set atmosphere.

Where to dine

  • Rossellinis — 1-Michelin-star restaurant at the Palazzo Avino, traditional cuisine reinterpreted with a light touch, served on a terrace suspended above the coast.

What to experience

  • Villa Rufolo — Medieval villa and gardens whose Belvedere, the stage of the Ravello Festival, offers a view ranked among the most famous in Italy over the Gulf of Salerno.
