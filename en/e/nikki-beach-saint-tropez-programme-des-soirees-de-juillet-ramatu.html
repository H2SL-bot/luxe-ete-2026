The turnkey stay

Built around a signature Nikki Beach happening on the sands of Pampelonne, this turnkey stay unfolds the full art de vivre of Saint-Tropez: nights in the hills of Ramatuelle facing the bay, toes-in-the-water lunches and exceptional dinners, then a party that carries on. Between pine groves, rosé vineyards and the Mediterranean, each day pairs chic idleness with Michelin-starred dining. The evening never fades: it lingers beneath the illuminated palms of Saint-Tropez until the early hours.

Where to stay

  • La Réserve Ramatuelle, Hôtel, Spa & Villas — A 5-star palace hotel on a private estate perched in the hills of Ramatuelle, facing the bay and just minutes from Pampelonne. Michelin-starred restaurant La Voile, a signature spa and its own private beach, La Réserve à la Plage. The ideal base from which to enjoy Nikki Beach.
  • Villa Marie Saint-Tropez — A 5-star hotel nestled in a 3-hectare pine grove in Ramatuelle, overlooking the legendary beaches of Pampelonne (approx. 4 km) and 3 km from the village. Provençal garden, infinity pool and a shuttle to Saint-Tropez: an intimate, refined address moments from the party.

Where to dine

  • Le Club 55 — A legendary institution on Pampelonne beach since 1955, on the same sands as Nikki Beach (43 Bd Patch, Ramatuelle). Mediterranean cuisine, wild-caught fish and crudités in an iconic setting: the quintessential Saint-Tropez lunch before or after the party.
  • La Vague d'Or, Cheval Blanc St-Tropez — The 3-Michelin-star gastronomic restaurant of chef Arnaud Donckele at Cheval Blanc St-Tropez, facing the sea. The peninsula's grandest table for a memorable dinner alongside the Pampelonne happenings.

What to experience

  • Château Minuty, visite de cave & dégustation — An emblematic wine estate in Gassin, one of the Crus Classés de Provence and home to one of the world's most celebrated rosés. Cellar visit and guided tasting of the cuvées just steps from Ramatuelle: the art de vivre of Saint-Tropez rosé.
  • Les Caves du Roy, Byblos Saint-Tropez — Saint-Tropez's legendary club since 1967, beneath its illuminated palm trees (27 Av. Foch, within the hotel Byblos). The place to carry the Nikki Beach evening through to dawn, in the most mythical party atmosphere on the Côte d'Azur.
