Sublime Summer Party 2026 — Twelve Memories to Tell

Thursday 6 August 2026, from 7.30 pm to midnight — and until 3 am at the Ruína club · Sublime Comporta – Sand, et le club Ruína (EN 261-1, Muda, Grândola) · Palace Hotels & Fine Dining

For its twelfth birthday, Sublime Comporta is holding its annual summer party in the resort's Sand wing, on the dunes of the Comporta. The party runs from 7.30 pm to midnight; holders of the Party & Club ticket then continue at the Ruína, from midnight to 3 am. Announced dress code: Summer Chic. The evening is open to families, with children aged 4 to 11 paying half price.

Line-up : Organised by Sublime Hotels, at its resort in the Comporta. Brings together the owners of the Sublime Sand villas and the hotel's international clientele.

How to get in

Sublime Hotels — reservations department of Sublime Comporta (EN 261-1, 7570-337 Muda, Grândola)

Two paying tickets: Sublime Party 2026 at 150 € (135 € for owners and hotel guests), access from 7.30 pm to midnight; Sublime Party & Club at 200 € (180 €), with access to the Ruína club from midnight to 3 am. Purchase online on the official page. To obtain the reduced rate, first book a night at the resort through the reservations department.

The turnkey stay

Entre rizières dorées, dunes sauvages et pins parasols, Comporta est le refuge estival le plus discret du jet-set européen. La Sublime Summer Party se tient sur place, dans le Fórum de Sublime Comporta — twelve years, twelve memories, une nuit d'élégance décontractée face à l'Atlantique.

Where to stay

  • Sublime Comporta Country Retreat & SPA — Le lieu même de l'événement : 23 chambres et 65 villas sur un domaine de 68 hectares entre pins et dunes, ambiance bohème-chic.
  • Quinta da Comporta – Wellness Boutique Resort — À Carvalhal, à quelques minutes : 61 chambres, spa Oryza, architecture signée Miguel Câncio Martins, esprit rustique-raffiné.

Where to dine

  • Restaurante SAL — Institution de Praia do Pego/Carvalhal, poissons ultra-frais de la côte, ambiance pieds dans le sable.

What to experience

  • Balade à cheval sur les dunes et rizières de Comporta — Excursion équestre organisée par Sublime Comporta sur les plages désertes et les rizières alentour.
