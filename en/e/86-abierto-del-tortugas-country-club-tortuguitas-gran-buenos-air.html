86° Abierto del Tortugas Country Club

From Tuesday 6 October to Saturday 7 November 2026 — group stage until 1 November, final on 7 November · Tortugas Country Club, Panamericana Ramal Pilar km 38.5, Tortuguitas · Polo · Motoring · Sailing

The second leg of Argentina's Triple Crown in 2026, the Abierto de Tortugas has been contested since 1930 at one of the most exclusive country clubs in Gran Buenos Aires, which presents itself as the country's first country club. The AAP's official calendar sets the group stage from 6 October to 1 November, with the final on 7 November. Polo is played here just a few metres from the spectators, in an atmosphere of family lunches and clipped hedges, a world away from mass grandstands.

Line-up : Organised by the Tortugas Country Club with the Asociación Argentina de Polo. A 2026 novelty confirmed by the AAP: teams coming from the qualifying tournament now play two legs of the Triple Crown, Tortugas and Palermo.

How to get in

Tortugas Country Club, jointly with the Asociación Argentina de Polo.

Purchase via Ticketek, through the Entradas section of the club's official website; for hospitality and reserved areas, go through the club's administration or the AAP.
