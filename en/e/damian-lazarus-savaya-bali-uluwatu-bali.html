Damian Lazarus — Savaya Bali

Saturday 21 November 2026, doors at 3pm · Savaya Bali, Jl. Belimbing Sari, Banjar Tambiyak, Pecatu, Uluwatu, Kabupaten Badung, Bali 80364 · Festivals & Music
✦✦✦✦ Radar score : 76/100

Savaya welcomes Damian Lazarus, founder of the Crosstown Rebels label and architect of the ritual nights of Day Zero, for a day that slides into night on its Uluwatu headland. Doors at 3pm; the party is restricted to guests over 21 on presentation of photo ID. Entry tickets, at 600,000 rupiah in early-bird pricing, are sold out, as is every other category.

Line-up : Damian Lazarus (Crosstown Rebels, Day Zero) headlining, joined by guest DJs throughout the day whose names have not been published. Organisation: Savaya Bali.

How to get in

Savaya Bali — the club's VIP and reservations team (official form and WhatsApp).

VIP table booking through the club's official form ("Savaya Bali Table Request") or its WhatsApp; tickets, all sold out, are neither refundable, nor convertible into a sofa, nor transferable.

The turnkey stay

On 21 November 2026, Damian Lazarus sets up his decks at Savaya, perched a hundred metres above the Indian Ocean on the limestone cliffs of Pecatu. Voted Asia's number one club and fifth worldwide in the DJ Mag Top 100 ranking, the venue lives to the rhythm of the sunset: the music rises as the light falls over the Indian Ocean, and never comes back down. All around, the Bukit peninsula unfurls its cliffs, its temples and its beaches below — enough to turn one night into a stay.

Where to stay

  • Bvlgari Resort Bali — On the same Uluwatu cliffs as the club (Jalan Goa Lempeh), atop the 160-metre limestone headland running along the southern coast of the Bukit. Villas and pavilions overlooking the sea, Bvlgari Spa, La Spiaggia beach below, a recently redesigned Serpenti Pool Club, and two signature tables: Il Ristorante — Niko Romito and Pier Daniele Seu's Pizza Bar. The Bvlgari Villa boasts a heated 20-metre pool that cascades into a second basin.
  • The Edge Bali — A private-villa resort in Pecatu, the very district of Savaya. Eight villas of one to five bedrooms, a spa suspended above the water and open to the ocean, the underground table The Cave by chef Ryan Clift — and above all oneeighty°, a day club whose glass-bottomed pool juts 6.5 metres into the void, 162 metres above the waves (figures given by the resort).

Where to dine

  • Il Ristorante, Niko Romito — High Italian cuisine signed by Italian chef Niko Romito, within Bvlgari Resort Bali on the Uluwatu cliff: the Bukit's most ambitious table on the gastronomy side.
  • Sundays Beach Club — In Ungasan, on the same Bukit peninsula. You descend the cliff aboard the club's private inclined lift (its "inclinator") to a beach where you dine facing the open sea, seafood to the fore. Live music from 5pm to 8pm and beach fires lit every evening at sunset. The sand-side table of The Ungasan Clifftop Resort.

What to experience

  • SGi Air Bali, vol privé en hélicoptère — An operator based at Benoa heliport (Denpasar), flying Balinese skies for more than twenty-five years and holder of the BARS Gold safety certification, the standard used by major international companies. VIP transfers, private charters and panoramic flights for up to six passengers, taking in the cliffs of Uluwatu, Nusa Penida and Mount Batur. Arriving on the Bukit by air rather than by road.
  • Yacht Sourcing, charter privé — A charter house based in Denpasar (Denpasar Selatan): motor yachts and traditional Indonesian phinisi, with crew. Outings from Bali towards Gili and Lombok, or long-range sailings to Komodo National Park and Raja Ampat. The day after the party, seen from the sea.
