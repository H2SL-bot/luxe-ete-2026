Sting Live Concert at Villa d'Este

Sunday 9 August 2026 · Jardin des Mosaïques, Villa d'Este, Via Regina 40, Cernobbio (Lac de Côme) · Festivals & Music

Villa d'Este offers its guests an evening of absolute rarity: Sting in concert before a deliberately limited audience, in the Mosaic Garden. The evening opens at 19:00 with an aperitif, continues at 20:00 with the concert, then closes at 21:30 with a gala dinner on the lakeside terrace. Everything unfolds a few metres from the façade of the palace hotel, facing the Lario.

Line-up : Sting, in live formation. Gala dinner prepared by the kitchens of Villa d'Este.

How to get in

The events and reservations office of Villa d'Este, which sells all of the seats.

Two official packages, both with accommodation: Premium from 11 600 € (two nights, gala dinner for two, two reserved seats) and Exclusive from 18 000 € (two nights in a suite, gala dinner for two, two seats in the Exclusive section). Booking online, on +39 031 443 80 11 or at rsvp@villadeste.it.

The turnkey stay

On 9 August 2026, Villa d'Este turns its Giardino del Mosaico into a theatre for a single evening: beneath a glass pavilion inspired by orangeries, raised against the Cardinal Building, Sting plays before a handful of guests. The aperitif opens at 19:00 on the Terrazza Platani facing the lake; at 21:30 the candlelit gala dinner is served on the Terrazza Lago, composed by executive chef Andrea Guerini. Between the terraced gardens of Cernobbio and the dark waters of the Lario, everything is in place for an evening with no second date.

Where to stay

  • Villa d'Este — The very setting of the concert. Historic lakeside palace hotel in Cernobbio: 151 rooms divided between the Cardinal Building and the Queen's Pavilion, the Veranda, Grill and Il Platano restaurants, Sporting Club with spa and fitness, private helipad. Two concert packages appear on the official site — two nights with breakfast, gala dinner for two and two seats: Premium from 11 600 €, Exclusive from 18 000 €, arrival on 8 or 9 August.
  • Passalacqua — In Moltrasio (Via Besana 59), a few minutes' drive north of Cernobbio, on the same western shore of the lake. A private residence since 1787 converted into a hotel, 24 rooms and suites, all different, in grounds running down to the water. Ranked 4th in The World's 50 Best Hotels and winner of the Best Boutique Hotel Award 2025.

Where to dine

  • Materia — One Michelin star awarded in 2018 and still in the guide, in the heart of Cernobbio (Via Trieste 1/b), a short distance from Villa d'Este. Cuisine by chef-owner Davide Caranchini. Watch the summer hours: from 28 June to 31 October 2026 the house opens only from Tuesday to Saturday. As 9 August falls on a Sunday, the restaurant is closed on the evening of the concert — to be booked the day before or the day after.
  • Il Gatto Nero — A long-established address in Cernobbio: indoor rooms furnished with works of art and a large panoramic terrace open onto the lake, renowned for its candlelit dinners. Booking through the website form or by telephone (+39 031 512042).

What to experience

  • Vol en hélicoptère au départ de l'hélisurface privée de Villa d'Este — Thirty minutes above the Alpine peaks, the historic villas and the waters of the lake, taking off from the hotel's private helipad — five to six passengers maximum. Variants offered: a Como and Lago Maggiore loop with a lunch stop (Il Sole di Ranco or Grand Hotel et des Iles Borromées), or a flight over Franciacorta followed by an estate visit at L'Albereta Bellavista. Booking through Villa d'Este Guest Relations; prepayment required, non-refundable.
  • Croisière privée en Riva d'époque, Como Classic Boats — Based in Laglio (Via Vecchia Regina 48), on the same western shore as Cernobbio, the operator fields eight boats: Riva Aquarama, Riva Tritone, Riva Ariston, Riva Aquariva, Venetian limousine, vaporina, e-vaporina and GUESS Boat. Private outings of one to six hours, skipper included, four to ten passengers depending on the craft.
