The Global Gift Gala Paris 2026

October 2026 — exact date, venue and prices not yet released by the organisers (the 2025 edition was held on 8 November) · Lieu non annoncé (édition 2025 : Four Seasons Hôtel George V, 31 av. George V, Paris 8e) · Festivals & Music
✦✦✦✦ Radar score : 80/100

Philanthropic gala founded by Maria Bravo, the Paris stop on an international circuit (Cannes, Marbella, London). Gala dinner, live performances and a charity auction bringing together celebrities and international patrons, red carpet. Tickets and tables bookable through the official office.

Line-up : 2026 line-up not yet announced. Previous editions (2025, press-verified): Eva Longoria, Maria Bravo, performances by Vitaa and M. Pokora, appearances by Kendji Girac, Slimane and Vincent Niclo.

How to get in

Global Gift Foundation

Official page still reads “Coming Soon”: contact the Global Gift Foundation office by e-mail or telephone as published on globalgiftgala.com to request a seat or a table; watch for ticketing to open at globalgiftgala.com/events/ (the other 2026 stops are already priced there: Marbella €1,000–5,000, Monaco €1,500–5,000).

The turnkey stay

In October 2026, the Golden Triangle dresses up for philanthropy. As the 2025 Global Gift Gala was held at the Four Seasons Hôtel George V, we anchor the stay on that exact point: everything is done on foot, from the palace hotels of avenue Montaigne to the most starred tables of the Right Bank, without ever getting back into a car. Since the exact date, venue and prices have not yet been published by the organiser, we hold flexible options and lock everything in the moment the official announcement lands.

Where to stay

  • Four Seasons Hôtel George V, Paris — 31 avenue George V, 75008 — The palace hotel that hosted the 2025 edition of the gala. Staying here means taking the lift down to the party: flower-filled courtyards by Jeff Leatham, suites with rooftop terraces, spa and overnight service. The safest address should the 2026 edition return to the same venue.
  • La Réserve Paris, Hotel & Spa — 42 avenue Gabriel, 75008 — Five-star palace hotel on the service lane of the Champs-Élysées, halfway between Faubourg Saint-Honoré and avenue Montaigne. Private-mansion spirit signed Jacques Garcia, just 40 rooms and suites, views of the Grand Palais and the Eiffel Tower: the discreet alternative, some ten minutes on foot from avenue George V.

Where to dine

  • Le Taillevent — 15 rue Lamennais, 75008 — Two Michelin stars, seven minutes on foot from avenue George V. An institution of Parisian classicism, carried by a cellar of more than 3,800 wines and spirits and food-and-wine pairings of rare precision. The dinner the night before — the one where the real conversations happen.
  • Pierre Gagnaire — 6 rue Balzac, 75008 — Chef Pierre Gagnaire's restaurant, a few streets from the George V. Signature cuisine built as series of small plates that answer one another: the address to book when you want an evening that surprises rather than one that reassures. Reserve very far ahead.

What to experience

  • Spa My Blend by Clarins, Le Royal Monceau, Raffles Paris — 37 avenue Hoche, 75008 — A beauty ritual on the afternoon of the gala: one of the largest palace-hotel spas in Paris, with a swimming pool and bespoke treatment rooms. You leave rested, fifteen minutes from the evening's venue.
  • Les Yachts de Paris, dîner privatisé sur la Seine — Yachts and private salons on the Seine, including the Don Juan II, where chef Frédéric Anton presides. The day after the gala: lunch or dinner at the quayside, then a cruise, illuminated monuments on either bank, a table set for your guests alone. The most elegant way to extend the evening without repeating it.
