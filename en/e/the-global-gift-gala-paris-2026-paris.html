The Global Gift Gala Paris 2026

October 2026 — exact date, venue and prices not yet released by the organisers (the 2025 edition was held on 8 November) · Lieu non annoncé (édition 2025 : Four Seasons Hôtel George V, 31 av. George V, Paris 8e) · Festivals & Music

Philanthropic gala founded by Maria Bravo, the Paris stop on an international circuit (Cannes, Marbella, London). Gala dinner, live performances and a charity auction bringing together celebrities and international patrons, red carpet. Tickets and tables bookable through the official office.

Line-up : 2026 line-up not yet announced. Previous editions (2025, press-verified): Eva Longoria, Maria Bravo, performances by Vitaa and M. Pokora, appearances by Kendji Girac, Slimane and Vincent Niclo.

How to get in

Global Gift Foundation

Official page still reads “Coming Soon”: contact the Global Gift Foundation office by e-mail or telephone as published on globalgiftgala.com to request a seat or a table; watch for ticketing to open at globalgiftgala.com/events/ (the other 2026 stops are already priced there: Marbella €1,000–5,000, Monaco €1,500–5,000).
