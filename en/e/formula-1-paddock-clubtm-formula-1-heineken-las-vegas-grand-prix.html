Formula 1 Paddock Club™ — Formula 1 Heineken Las Vegas Grand Prix 2026

Thursday 19 to Saturday 21 November 2026 · Las Vegas Strip Circuit — Paddock Club, le long du Koval Straight, Las Vegas Strip, Nevada · Polo · Motoring · Sailing

The Paddock Club is the most exclusive enclosure at the Las Vegas Grand Prix, a night race held on a street circuit laid out along the Strip itself: 6.201 km, seventeen corners, fifty laps for a race distance of 309.958 km. The programme is marketed under the name Paddock Club™ through Formula 1's two official channels: the championship's hospitality ticketing service and F1 Experiences, its authorised reseller, which lists it in its 2026 catalogue alongside the Legend, SkyBox and Turn 3 Club packages. Named tickets only; no box-office sales.

Line-up : The teams and drivers of the 2026 grid. No named appearance is guaranteed in writing on the official pages consulted: to be clarified at the time of booking.

How to get in

Formula 1 — official hospitality ticketing; F1 Experiences (Quint Events), official reseller of Formula 1 hospitality programmes.

Purchase from Formula 1's official hospitality ticketing service (assistance +1 718-682-7493), or from F1 Experiences, which lists the Paddock Club™ in its 2026 catalogue for Las Vegas (+1.888.326.5430 in the United States, +44 203 770 3909 in the United Kingdom). Prices on request. The exact details of the services included must be confirmed at the time of booking.
