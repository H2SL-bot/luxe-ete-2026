68th Summer Party of the Southampton Hospital Foundation

1 August 2026 · Sous tentes, Wickapogue Road, Southampton · Other

The oldest gala in the Hamptons (68th edition), themed 1940s Supper Club, chaired by Lynn A. Scotti, in aid of Stony Brook Southampton Hospital and the Stony Brook East Hampton Emergency Department. The 2025 edition (67th) raised more than $1.6M.

Line-up : The great philanthropic families of Southampton, honorary chairs of New York society

The turnkey stay

Every summer for 68 years, the first Saturday of August has seen the East End jet set converge on the illuminated tents raised at the corner of Wickapogue Road and Old Town Road: the Southampton Hospital Foundation's Summer Party is the grande dame of Hamptons galas. On August 1, 2026, from 6 p.m., a refined dinner, the Frank Simmons Band and a dance floor going late into the night, in support of Stony Brook Southampton Hospital and East Hampton's emergency care. Tables are snapped up from 15,000 dollars — to be under those tents that evening is to claim the very peak of the Southampton summer.

Where to stay

  • Topping Rose House — The Hamptons' only full-service grand luxury hotel: a 19th-century mansion reimagined in the heart of Bridgehampton, some ten minutes' drive from the Wickapogue Road tents. Twenty-two rooms and suites, a Farmaesthetics spa, a pool, an adjoining kitchen farm — and Jean-Georges Vongerichten's restaurant on the ground floor.
  • Canoe Place Inn & Cottages — Built on the site of America's oldest inn, on the Shinnecock Canal in Hampton Bays, about fifteen minutes west of the event. A restored inn, cottages with patios and fire pits, waterfront townhomes, a pool, the ONDA spa and the Good Ground Tavern restaurant.

Where to dine

  • Nick & Toni's — East Hampton's Italian-Mediterranean institution since 1988, the unapologetic canteen of East End celebrities. Around the wood-burning oven, the kitchen exalts local farms — house-made pappardelle, whole roasted fish — and the table remains more coveted than any other on an August Saturday night.
  • Sant Ambroeus Southampton — The Hamptons outpost of the legendary Milanese house, at 30 Main Street in Southampton, minutes from Wickapogue Road. Milanese cooking, an espresso bar, house-made gelato and a vine-covered patio away from the bustle — the quintessential pre-gala lunch.

What to experience

  • Transfert en hélicoptère BLADE Manhattan – Southampton — Reach the Hamptons as one should: roughly 35 to 40 minutes' flight between Manhattan and the Hamptons, by helicopter or seaplane, in the summer season. By-the-seat flights and private charters on request, booked via the BLADE app or website.
  • Dégustation au Wölffer Estate Vineyard — In Sagaponack, some ten minutes east of Wickapogue Road, the family estate founded in 1988 sustainably farms 55 acres of vines. Tastings in the Tasting Room facing the vineyard and at the open-air Wine Stand — the emblematic rosé of the Hamptons summer, at sunset.
