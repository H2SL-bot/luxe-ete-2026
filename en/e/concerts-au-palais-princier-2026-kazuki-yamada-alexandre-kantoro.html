Concerts au Palais Princier 2026 — Kazuki Yamada & Alexandre Kantorow (OPMC, season closing)

6 August 2026, 9.30pm · Cour d'Honneur du Palais Princier de Monaco · Art & Auctions

Closing concert of the 2026 season of the Concerts au Palais Princier and Kazuki Yamada's final concert as artistic and musical director of the OPMC before his departure from Monaco. A Liszt (Piano Concerto No. 2) / Ravel programme with Alexandre Kantorow, winner of the 2019 Tchaikovsky Competition. Listed SOLD OUT (waiting list) on the official website from July 2026.

Line-up : Kazuki Yamada (conducting, final concert as artistic and musical director of the OPMC), Alexandre Kantorow (piano)

How to get in

Orchestre Philharmonique de Monte-Carlo (OPMC), closing concert of the 2026 season of the Concerts at the Princely Palace and Kazuki Yamada's final concert as artistic and music director of the OPMC; under the high patronage of the Princely Palace, in the Palace's Cour d'Honneur

Concert listed SOLD OUT (waiting list) on the official site as of July 2026. Realistic route: join the waiting list via the OPMC box office (Atrium du Casino counter, +377 92 00 13 70) or opmc.mc / montecarloticket.com, and watch for last-minute releases. Face-value prices from 20 € to 175 € (Carré d'or). Formal town dress required, jacket and tie mandatory for men. Press route: accreditation with the OPMC administration (info@opmc.mc, +377 93 10 85 00) — a landmark evening (Kazuki Yamada's final concert, with Alexandre Kantorow).

The turnkey stay

An emotionally charged closing evening: Kazuki Yamada conducts his final concert as music director of the OPMC, alongside French pianist Alexandre Kantorow in a programme of Liszt and Ravel. The Cour d'Honneur of the Palais Princier, illuminated for the occasion, provides a uniquely historic setting for this highlight of the summer season. A moment of great music already sold out, a symbol of Monegasque cultural prestige. An evening to experience between artistic fervour and princely elegance.

Where to stay

  • Hôtel de Paris Monte-Carlo — Historic 1864 palace hotel on Place du Casino, a short distance from the Rocher and the Palais Princier, renowned for its Belle Époque architecture and exceptional suites.
  • Hôtel Hermitage Monte-Carlo — Palace hotel on Square Beaumarchais with a glass canopy designed by Gustave Eiffel, near the Casino and old Monaco, combining Belle Époque splendour with contemporary comfort.

Where to dine

  • Le Louis XV - Alain Ducasse à l'Hôtel de Paris — Three-Michelin-star restaurant by Alain Ducasse, the benchmark for Mediterranean gastronomy in Monaco since 1987, located on Place du Casino.
  • Le Grill — Starred restaurant atop the Hôtel de Paris, with a retractable roof, refined grills and a sweeping view over Place du Casino and the sea.

What to experience

  • Musée océanographique de Monaco — Institution founded in 1910 on the Rocher by Prince Albert I, near the Palais Princier; aquariums, a shark lagoon and scientific collections.
  • Jardin Exotique de Monaco — Botanical garden perched on the cliff since 1931, with panoramic views over the Principality, a prehistoric cave and an anthropology museum.
