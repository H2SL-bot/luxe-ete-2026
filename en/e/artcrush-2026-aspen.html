ArtCrush 2026

Friday, July 31, 2026 · Base de Buttermilk Mountain, Aspen (organisateur : Aspen Art Museum, 637 East Hyman Avenue, Aspen, Colorado 81611) · Festivals & Music

The Aspen Art Museum's grand annual gala, staged under a tent at the base of Buttermilk Mountain. The evening honors artist Adrian Villar Rojas and is held under the co-chairmanship of Sarah Arison and Charlie Pohlad. It is presented by ZEGNA, with Van Cleef & Arpels among the leading patrons. An after-party follows the same evening, from 9pm to 11:59pm.

Line-up : Adrian Villar Rojas, honored artist of the 2026 edition. Co-chairs: Sarah Arison and Charlie Pohlad. Evening presented by ZEGNA, with Van Cleef & Arpels among the patrons.

How to get in

Aspen Art Museum — ArtCrush office (development and patronage)

Write to artcrush@aspenartmuseum.org or call 970.925.8050 to reserve a seat or a patron table. The after-party is booked separately, through the museum's online box office.

The turnkey stay

On July 31, 2026, the base of Buttermilk Mountain hosts ArtCrush, the Aspen Art Museum's grand annual gathering: a gala and auction, the museum's largest fundraiser, whose proceeds support its exhibitions and education programs. Works in the live auction are on offer from July 14 to 31, 2026, with the online auction running from July 21 to August 1. In the lead-up, the museum, at 637 East Hyman Avenue, remains open free of charge, two blocks from the palace hotels and great tables of downtown Aspen.

Where to stay

  • The Little Nell — At 675 East Durant Avenue, at the base of Aspen Mountain: the official site presents it as the only hotel in Aspen to hold both Forbes Five-Star and AAA Five Diamond ratings, and it is a member of Relais & Châteaux. Two blocks from the Aspen Art Museum, a few minutes' drive from Buttermilk. On site, The Spa at The Little Nell and the restaurant Element 47, recognized by the Michelin Guide, whose wine list exceeds 600 references.
  • Hotel Jerome, Auberge Resorts Collection — Opened in 1889 at 330 East Main Street and listed on the National Register of Historic Places since 1986, the Jerome is Aspen's historic house, today operated by Auberge Resorts Collection. Its J-Bar, born with the hotel, remains a local institution. In the heart of downtown, a few blocks from the museum and a few minutes' drive from Buttermilk.

Where to dine

  • Bosq — One Michelin star — the restaurant presents itself as Aspen's first and only starred table since the Guide arrived in Colorado — under chef-owner Barclay Dodge. A "forest to table" cuisine fed by his own foraging: porcini gathered in the forests of Independence Pass, spruce tips, wild rose, watercress, serviceberry branches. At 312 South Mill Street, in the heart of Aspen, a few blocks from the museum.
  • Matsuhisa Aspen — Chef Nobu Matsuhisa's Japanese table in Aspen, at 303 East Main Street, on the same block as the Hotel Jerome and a few minutes' walk from the museum. The official site publishes no opening hours: reservations to be confirmed directly with the restaurant (970 544 6628).

What to experience

  • Aspen Art Museum, visite avant le gala — The museum, at 637 East Hyman Avenue, is open Tuesday through Sunday from 10am to 6pm (closed Monday) and admission is free, courtesy of Amy and John Phelan. This is where the ArtCrush auction takes place (live from July 14 to 31, 2026, online from July 21 to August 1). On site, the Swedish Hill café, on level 3, Tuesday through Sunday from 9am to 2pm.
  • Remède Spa, The St. Regis Aspen Resort — The spa of the St. Regis Aspen Resort, 315 East Dean Street, at the base of Aspen Mountain and steps from the Little Nell and the museum. Services and availability to be confirmed directly with the hotel before booking.
