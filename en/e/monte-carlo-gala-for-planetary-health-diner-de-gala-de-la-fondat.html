Monte-Carlo Gala for Planetary Health — the Prince Albert II Foundation gala dinner

The 2026 edition is expected in September (recurring) — official date NOT yet published as of 17 July 2026; late September is an estimate, to be confirmed on montecarlogala.org / fpa2.org · Monaco — lieu à confirmer (éditions passées au Palais Princier / Musée océanographique / cadre princier SBM) · Festivals & Music

The flagship charity gala dinner of the Prince Albert II of Monaco Foundation (benefiting planetary health and the Princess Charlene Foundation), traditionally held in September. Past editions have drawn a global star line-up — DiCaprio, Nicole Kidman, Madonna, Eva Longoria, Orlando Bloom — with dinner, award presentations and a concert. Access by table or donation through the Foundation. Please note: the 2026 date has not yet been officially announced (late September is an estimate, to be confirmed).

Line-up : VERIFIED track record (past editions): Leonardo DiCaprio, Nicole Kidman, Madonna, Eva Longoria, Orlando Bloom, Robert Redford. The 2026 line-up has not yet been announced.

How to get in

table / seat / charitable donation (gala ticketing through the Foundation)

Official route: contact the Prince Albert II of Monaco Foundation (FPA2) to purchase a seat/table or make a donation — email planetgala@fpa2.org or tel. +377 98 98 81 01. Follow the announcement of the 2026 date and ticketing on montecarlogala.org and fpa2.org.
