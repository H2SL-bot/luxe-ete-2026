Chaumet opens 12 place Vendôme, LVMH Journées Particulières

From Friday 16 to Sunday 18 October 2026, during the LVMH Journées Particulières. The "Diadèmes de Rêve" visit is free, on reservation; bookings open in the course of September 2026. · L'Hôtel Particulier de Chaumet, 12 place Vendôme, 75001 Paris · Jewellery & Watchmaking

For the 6th edition of the LVMH Journées Particulières, Chaumet opens its hôtel particulier at 12 place Vendôme, the maison's home since 1812: the "Diadèmes de Rêve" experience leads behind the scenes of creation, from gold and gemstones to the finished diadem, in salons usually reserved for high jewellery clients.

Access

Free admission on reservation via the official Journées Particulières website (lvmh.com); bookings open in the course of September 2026. Wheelchair accessible; minimum age 6.
