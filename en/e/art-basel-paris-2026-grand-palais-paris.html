Art Basel Paris 2026 — Grand Palais

From October 21 to 25, 2026 · Grand Palais (VIP preview 21-22, public 23-25) · Grand Palais · Art & Auctions
✓ Verified at the source on 16/07/2026

The jet-set rendezvous of the Paris autumn: 206 galleries from 41 countries under the nave of the Grand Palais for the 5th edition, the first led by Karim Crippa. The VIP days on October 21-22 gather billionaire collectors, luxury houses and celebrities ahead of the public opening.

Line-up : Major international collectors, leading galleries, partner luxury houses

How to get in

Art Basel (MCH Group) — Paris edition at the Grand Palais

The free route goes through the galleries: a VIP preview invitation is obtained from a participating gallery where you are a client — or a serious prospect. Otherwise, Premium tickets (vernissage on October 22) and public tickets are on sale at artbasel.com. Press accreditation opens the VIP days to journalists who can show a media affiliation.

The turnkey stay

Five days in the heart of the Golden Triangle: two palace hotels a few steps from the Grand Palais, Michelin-starred tables in the Champs-Élysées gardens, and private escapes on the Seine and above Paris.

Where to stay

  • Le Bristol Paris — France's first palace hotel, on rue du Faubourg Saint-Honoré: a vast inner garden, a rooftop pool facing the Paris rooftops, and Epicure, three Michelin stars.
  • Hôtel de Crillon, A Rosewood Hotel — An 18th-century residence on Place de la Concorde, a ten-minute walk from the Grand Palais: signature suites, the Sense spa, and the Nonos restaurant.

Where to dine

  • Alléno Paris au Pavillon Ledoyen — Yannick Alléno's three Michelin stars in a neoclassical pavilion in the Champs-Élysées gardens, moments from the Grand Palais.
  • Monsieur Bleu — The Palais de Tokyo's restaurant, open onto the Seine and the Eiffel Tower: contemporary French cuisine, a spectacular dining room, and a seasonal garden.

What to experience

  • Yachts de Paris — Private hire of a yacht on the Seine, from the Cachemire to the Paquebot, with a gastronomic dinner served on board as the monuments glide past.
  • AEROAFFAIRES, hélicoptère privé, héliport de Paris — Private helicopter charter from the Paris heliport: airport transfers, flights over the region, and excursions to châteaux and estates.
