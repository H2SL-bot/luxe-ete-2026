Zamna Bali

Saturday 17 and Sunday 18 October 2026 · Savaya Bali, Jl. Belimbing Sari, Banjar Tambiyak, Pecatu, Uluwatu, Kabupaten Badung, Bali 80364 · Festivals & Music

Zamna, the festival brand born in Tulum, takes over the Savaya promontory in Uluwatu for two days — a venue the house claims as Asia's number one club and fifth worldwide in the DJ Mag Top 100 Clubs ranking. Doors open at 3pm on Saturday and 1pm on Sunday, with entry strictly reserved for those over 21 upon presentation of ID. All ticket categories, from the single pass at 800,000 rupiah to the two-day pass at 1,000,000 rupiah, are sold out. Only tables remain negotiable, and the club notes that capacity is limited to tables and ticket holders.

Line-up : Line-up curated by Zamna (the brand born in Tulum). The club announces several DJs playing through the day up to the headliners, but has published no artist names to date. Organised by Savaya Bali.

How to get in

Savaya Bali — the club's VIP and reservations team (official form and WhatsApp), official Megatix ticketing.

Book a VIP table via the club's official form (« Savaya Bali Table Request ») or its WhatsApp; standard tickets, all sold out, were sold on the dedicated Megatix ticketing platform.

The turnkey stay

On 17 and 18 October 2026, Zamna — born in the Tulum jungle — settles at Savaya, the cliff enclave suspended one hundred metres above the Indian Ocean in Uluwatu. Two days of house and techno tipping into the night, around the Cube from which the sunset sets are launched, in the club voted No. 1 in Asia and No. 5 worldwide in the DJ Mag Top 100 Clubs. Doors at 3pm on Saturday, 1pm on Sunday, strictly 21 and over; tickets are bought on the club's website, per day or as a two-day pass. And since you're coming all the way to the Bukit, you might as well live it in full — cliff, table and private cove included.

Where to stay

  • Alila Villas Uluwatu — Jalan Belimbing Sari, Banjar Tambiyak, Desa Pecatu 80364 — the very street of Savaya, a few hundred metres from the club: you return from the festival without leaving the cliff plateau. Villas with private pools, the Cire restaurant and Sunset Cabana facing the ocean, spa and wellness centre, gym, airport shuttle. Bookable for the nights of 17 and 18 October 2026.
  • Bvlgari Resort Bali — Jalan Goa Lempeh, Banjar Dinas Kangin, Uluwatu — perched on the same 160-metre limestone cliff of the Bukit as Savaya, on the Uluwatu temple side, about 2.5 km from the club. Villas with private pools, a private beach below, a spa with steam bath and couples' treatments, yoga classes, and Il Ristorante – Niko Romito on site. Bookable for the nights of 17 and 18 October 2026.

Where to dine

  • Il Ristorante – Niko Romito, Bvlgari Resort Bali — At the heart of the Bvlgari Resort Bali, on the 160-metre limestone cliff of Jalan Goa Lempeh, in Uluwatu. A concept by Niko Romito, chef of Reale (three Michelin stars in Italy), who remains consultant and oversees the kitchen; service on site is led by chef Francesca Stabile. Dinner only, from 6pm to 10pm (last order at 9pm), resort smart casual dress code, children under five not admitted in the dining room. Note: the Michelin guide publishes no selection in Indonesia — the house is therefore not starred.
  • Cuca — In Jimbaran, on the road to the Bukit: the institution opened in 2013 by Canadian chef Kevin Cherkas, formerly of elBulli, Arzak, La Broche and Daniel. Signature tapas built on Indonesian produce, cocktails and desserts, a Chef Tasting Meal, open daily from 12pm to 11pm. The Michelin guide does not award stars in Indonesia; the house appears in the Michelin Green Guide 2025 and in Tatler's rankings of Indonesia's best tables for 2025 and 2026.

What to experience

  • Vols et transferts privés en hélicoptère – SGi Air Bali — Private helicopter transfer to the Bukit, or flights over the Uluwatu cliffs, Nusa Penida and Mount Batur. SGi Air Bali is Bali's historic helicopter operator — it "opened the Balinese skies more than 25 years ago" — certified BARS Gold and honoured by HAI (Salute to Excellence and Operator Safety Award). Scenic flights, VIP transfers, private charters and day excursions, up to 6 passengers per aircraft.
  • Sundays Beach Club, The Ungasan Clifftop Resort — The festival's counter-programme, about 1 km from Savaya: a white-sand cove at the foot of the Ungasan cliff (Jalan Pantai Selatan Gau, Banjar Wijaya Kusuma), part of The Ungasan Clifftop Resort, reached by a private inclinator. Kayaking, paddleboarding and snorkelling in the afternoon, live music from 5pm to 8pm, a bonfire every evening at 6:30pm, BBQ Asado on Wednesdays and Sundays from 2pm to 6pm, and cinema with your feet in the sand.
