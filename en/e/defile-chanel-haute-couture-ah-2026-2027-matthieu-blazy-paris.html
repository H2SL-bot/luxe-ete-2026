Chanel Haute Couture Show FW 2026-2027 (Matthieu Blazy)

7 July 2026, 10:00 and 12:00 (two shows) · Grand Palais, avenue Winston-Churchill, Paris 8e · Fashion & Couture

Matthieu Blazy's second haute couture collection for Chanel, presented in two shows at the Grand Palais transformed into a dark fairy tale (giant beanstalks, oversized flowers), inspired by a book of fairy tales found in Gabrielle Chanel's apartment.

Line-up : Matthieu Blazy (artistic director); Tilda Swinton, Michelle Yeoh, Catherine Deneuve in the front row

How to get in

CHANEL (Fashion Division) — show produced in-house at the Grand Palais, listed on the official calendar of the Fédération de la Haute Couture et de la Mode (FHCM), wee…

No seats sold: no ticketing, no charity table, no documented concierge access. The realistic route is to become a haute couture client: build a relationship with a Chanel boutique, then secure an appointment at the haute couture salons at 31 rue Cambon (by appointment only); leading couture clients are invited to the 10am or 12pm show. Otherwise: press accreditation via the press office (a justified outlet), or the film of the show the same day on chanel.com and standing outside the Grand Palais.

The turnkey stay

From Matthieu Blazy's dark fairy tale at the Grand Palais to the gilding of a neighbouring palace: your couture stay, steps from the show, orchestrated down to the millimetre.

Where to stay

  • La Réserve Paris — Hotel and Spa — 5* palace on avenue Gabriel, a few minutes' walk from the Grand Palais. Jacques Garcia decor, the 3-star Le Gabriel dining room.
  • Hôtel de Crillon, A Rosewood Hotel — Rosewood palace on place de la Concorde, at the foot of the Champs-Élysées, steps from the show. Sense spa and indoor pool.

Where to dine

  • Alléno Paris au Pavillon Ledoyen — Yannick Alléno's 3 Michelin stars, in the gardens of the Champs-Élysées, literally next door to the Grand Palais.
  • Epicure — Le Bristol Paris — Eric Frechon's 3 Michelin stars, dining room opening onto the formal French garden, minutes from the show.

What to experience

  • Spa Le Bristol by La Prairie — 440 m² of signature La Prairie treatments, 8 light-filled cabins opening onto the garden, at the heart of the palace.
  • Sense, A Rosewood Spa — Crillon — Indoor pool, hammam and bespoke treatments in the intimacy of the place de la Concorde palace.
