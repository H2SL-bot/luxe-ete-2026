Besch Cannes Auction summer sales (Hôtel Martinez)

13 to 16 August 2026 · Hôtel Martinez, 45 La Croisette, Cannes · Art & Auctions

Besch Cannes Auction's traditional summer sales at the Martinez, the PACA reference for fine jewellery and prestige wines; public viewings of the lots ahead of the sales (catalogues forthcoming).

How to get in

Besch Cannes Auction, an auction house founded in 1989 (Jean-Pierre Besch), located at 45 La Croisette in Cannes; the summer sales are held in the salons of the Hôtel Martinez.

Program of summer sales at the Martinez (August 13-16, 2026): Grands Vins & Alcools, Arts Passion, Diamants/Bijoux/Montres. Free route: free admission to the public exhibitions of lots and to the sales — you can attend the room without buying. To bid: in person at the Martinez, by phone after registering with the auction house (provide identification and an absentee bid in advance), or live on Drouot.com / Interencheres.com. Catalogues downloadable free of charge on cannesauction.com as soon as they are released; contact the expert for the relevant sale for an estimate or an absentee bid.
