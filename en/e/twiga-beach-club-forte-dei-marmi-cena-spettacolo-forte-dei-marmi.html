Twiga Beach Club Forte dei Marmi — cena spettacolo

Summer 2026 season (Vesta cena spettacolo dinner-shows and Twiga dance nights) — the season actually opens as early as April 3, with the heart of summer running June to September. · Twiga Beach Club / Vesta Versilia : front de mer · Festivals & Music

The most exclusive beach club in Versilia, born from an idea by Flavio Briatore and now signed by LMDV (Leonardo Maria Del Vecchio) under the name Vesta. By night, a dinner-show with live performances sets the stage for the dancing that follows, driven by the finest Italian and international DJs — the very archetype of the jet-set evening, between umbrella pines and the sea.

Line-up : Exceptional guests and DJs by calendar (themed evenings, live guests) — line-up published as the season unfolds.

How to get in

Twiga Versilia / Vesta Versilia (LMDV Hospitality)

Access by booking a cena-spettacolo (arrival around 21:00–22:00) or a tavolo dopo-cena (after 00:30) via the official site twigafortedeimarmi.com; ticketing only for special events. Many evenings sell out well in advance.

The turnkey stay

After the cena spettacolo at the water's edge, the Versilian night continues without ever leaving the Forte's art of living: palaces with their feet in the sand, Michelin-starred tables facing the Tyrrhenian and slow mornings between private beach and wellness rooftop. Here everything is a stroll away — the pale sand, the pine groves and the Forte market — for a stay where the evening's exception becomes the colour of every day. A Tuscan interlude where luxury is lived discreet, sun-drenched and refined.

Where to stay

  • Hotel Byron (Relais & Châteaux, 5 étoiles) — An intimate Belle Époque villa facing the sea, a Relais & Châteaux member, with pool, private garden and the gastronomic restaurant La Magnolia — the most elegant and confidential address on the seafront.
  • Principe Forte dei Marmi (The Leading Hotels of the World, 5 étoiles) — A contemporary palace on Viale Morin, a member of Leading Hotels of the World, with the Egoista spa, the 67 Sky Lounge rooftop and the starred restaurant Lux Lucis — the Forte's grand seaside luxury.

Where to dine

  • Ristorante Lorenzo (1 étoile Michelin) — A Forte institution since 1981, a temple of Tyrrhenian fish with its mayonnaise prepared tableside and a cellar of nearly 2,000 labels — Via Carducci 61, a stone's throw from the centre.
  • Bistrot Forte dei Marmi (1 étoile Michelin) — The Michelin-starred table of the Vaiani family led by chef Andrea Mattei, contemporary sea cuisine and wood-fired ovens, on Viale Franceschi facing the beach.

What to experience

  • Bagno Alpemare, beach club d'Andrea Bocelli — Italy's most sought-after private beach, envisioned by the Bocelli family: fully equipped tent-cabanas, impeccable service, a seaside restaurant and Guerlain Plage — a day of exceptional beach life on the Forte's sand.
  • Egoista Spa, Principe Forte dei Marmi — A panoramic 400 m² spa with heated pool, saunas, hammam and the 67 Sky Lounge rooftop for aperitivo at sunset facing the Apuan Alps — the Forte's signature wellness ritual.
