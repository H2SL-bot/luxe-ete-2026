Les Caves du Roy (Hôtel Byblos) — summer season, open every evening in July

26 June - 29 August 2026 (every evening in July-August) · Hôtel Byblos, avenue Paul Signac, Saint-Tropez · Palace Hotels & Fine Dining
✦✦✦ Radar score : 68/100

The legendary Saint-Tropez nightclub (since 1967), open every evening from Friday 26 June to Saturday 29 August 2026, with resident DJ Jack.E and international guests. A celebrity rendezvous after the peninsula's dinners. Confirmed calendar: weekends in spring, then daily opening at the height of summer.

Line-up : Resident DJ Jack.E; regular celebrity appearances in July-August

How to get in

Hôtel Byblos — Les Caves du Roy, an establishment of the family-owned Floirat Signatures group (Groupe Floirat)

No invitation to secure: paid entry filtered by the doorman. The royal route: book a table via the online module at lescavesduroy.com, by telephone or through the Byblos concierge (staying at the hotel makes entry considerably easier). Minimums are not officially published; specialist guides cite ~1 500 EUR on weekdays, ~10 000 EUR for 10 people, up to 30 000 EUR for the royal tables in August. Book at least a month in advance for July-August. Elegant attire required.

The turnkey stay

Extend the magic of Les Caves du Roy by making the village of Saint-Tropez your setting. Between palace hotels facing the Mediterranean, exceptional tables and the golden sand of Pampelonne, every hour becomes an art of living: crystalline mornings by the water, lunches with your feet in the sand, tastings in the heart of the vineyards of the Gulf. Here the party never truly ends — it is savoured, from the last glass at the Byblos to sunrise over the bay.

Where to stay

  • Airelles Saint-Tropez, Château de la Messardière — A 5-star palace hotel perched on the heights, set in 13 hectares of umbrella pines with panoramic sea views. Valmont–Cinq Mondes spa, tables signed Nobu (Matsuhisa) and Jean-François Piège. Just minutes from the Byblos.
  • Hôtel de Paris Saint-Tropez — A 5-star palace hotel in the heart of the village since 1931. Saint-Tropez's only rooftop with Mediterranean views, a Spa by Clarins and the private beach La Serena at Pampelonne. Steps from the Byblos and Les Caves du Roy.

Where to dine

  • La Vague d'Or, Cheval Blanc St-Tropez — Chef Arnaud Donckele's three-Michelin-starred gastronomic restaurant, on the port facing the baie des Canoubiers. A symphony of Provençal flavours and legendary sauces: the pinnacle of Saint-Tropez gastronomy. Evenings, except Wednesdays.
  • Restaurant La Ponche — A Saint-Tropez institution born in 1949 in the heart of the fishermen's quarter. Mediterranean cuisine with Provençal accents (chef Simon Pinault), with a terrace open to the sea. The intimate, chic soul of Saint-Tropez, minutes from the Byblos.

What to experience

  • Club 55, plage privée de Pampelonne — Pampelonne's legendary private beach since 1955 (43 Bd Patch, Ramatuelle). Wild-caught fish and lunches with your feet in the sand — the most iconic seaside address on the peninsula. Open daily, 10 am–5 pm.
  • Château Minuty, visite & dégustation (Gassin) — A Cru Classé de Provence estate in Gassin, owned by the Matton family for more than 80 years. Tour the estate and taste the iconic cuvées (M de Minuty, 281) in the heart of the vineyards of the Gulf of Saint-Tropez. Wine tourism by reservation.
