Nammos Baja Sardinia — 2026 beach club season (new opening)

2026 season open since 29 May; beach 11:00–18:00, restaurant 12:30–01:00; recurring DJ nights July–September (note: headliner dates announced as the season unfolds) · Nammos Baja Sardinia, Phi Beach : Via Forte Cappelini, 07021 Arzachena (Baja Sardinia, Costa Smeralda), front de mer sous les rochers de granit · Festivals & Music

Nammos' first Sardinian outpost — the jet-set beach-club brand born at Psarou (Mykonos) and since rolled out to Dubai, Cannes and Limassol. A restaurant at the water's edge between granite boulders and the Tyrrhenian Sea, Mediterranean sharing plates, magnums of champagne, and a mood that drifts from sun-drenched lunch to festive late tables. The same see-and-be-seen DNA as Nammos Mykonos.

Line-up : World-class DJ line-up announced for the 2026 Phi Beach/Nammos season: Black Coffee, Peggy Gou, Jamie Jones, Damian Lazarus, Claptone, Michael Bibi, Seth Troxler, Bob Sinclar, Kungs, Argy, Themba, among others. International superyacht and jet-set crowd.

The turnkey stay

Baja Sardinia is the Costa Smeralda stripped of Porto Cervo's protocol: wind-sculpted granite blocks, a sea that turns turquoise at the very first cove, and the La Maddalena archipelago resting on the horizon. Nammos settles in for the season beneath the rocks of Phi Beach — sun lounger and lunch at the water's edge until 18:00, then a dinner that slips gently into the night until 01:00. All around, a quarter of an hour is enough to go from the coast's only starred table to a Vermentino cellar lost in the Gallura maquis.

Where to stay

  • 7Pines Resort Sardinia, A Destination by Hyatt — The first Italian address in the Destination by Hyatt portfolio, set on 15 hectares of fragrant gardens at Loc. Li Mucchi Bianchi, Baja Sardinia (07021 Arzachena) — a few minutes from Phi Beach. 75 suites and rooms nestled among the granite formations, private coves and a jetty, infinity pool facing the La Maddalena marine protected area, Pure Seven Spa around a central patio. Three restaurants: Capogiro, Spazio, Cone Club. Porto Cervo 10 minutes away, Olbia 35 km.
  • L'Ea Bianca Luxury Resort — An intimate five-star on Cala dei Ginepri, 07020 Baja Sardinia — minutes from the village and from Phi Beach. 34 luminous rooms blending Sardinian and Middle Eastern influences, plus 5 villas for those who want complete discretion. Private beach on the cove, infinity pool opening onto the bay. Tel. +39 0789 974311. The right choice when you prefer the scale of a house to that of a resort.

Where to dine

  • Italo Bassi ConFusion Restaurant, Porto Cervo — The only Michelin star in Porto Cervo and in the whole Costa Smeralda, confirmed in the 2026 Italy guide. Italo Bassi, forty years in the trade, presides over an open kitchen on the Promenade du Port, facing the old harbour (Via Aga Khan 1, 07021 Porto Cervo). Two tasting menus — the house classics or the new creations, in several lengths — or à la carte, with oysters, caviar and the day's catch served in their purest form. 2026 season: 23 April to 29 September. Reservations +39 340 120 9574 or info@confusion-restaurant.com. About 20 minutes from Baja Sardinia.
  • Matsuhisa at Cala di Volpe, Hotel Cala di Volpe, a Luxury Collection Hotel — Nobu Matsuhisa's Sardinian outpost, inside the legendary Costa Smeralda hotel designed by Jacques Couëlle (Porto Cervo, about 20 minutes from Baja Sardinia). Japanese precision with Peruvian accents — black cod with miso, tiradito — facing the bay of Cala di Volpe. The house also holds the open-air Barbecue beside one of the largest seawater pools in the Mediterranean, plus Beefbar and Le Grand for Sardinian and Italian cuisine. Book through the hotel concierge.

What to experience

  • Vigne Surrau, cave et dégustation en Gallura (Arzachena) — A contemporary-architecture winery on the S.P. Porto Cervo–Arzachena, km 1, about 10 minutes from Baja Sardinia. Founded in 2000, the estate spans 50 hectares of vines across 7 zones of the Arzachena district: Vermentino and Cannonau to the fore, rounded out by passiti and a classic-method sparkling. The visit runs from a walk through the vines to grasp the Gallura terroir, to a tour of the cantina from harvest to bottling, a contemporary art exhibition, then a tasting of five wines with Sardinian breads, cheeses and charcuterie. By reservation (info@surrau.it, +39 0789 82933). Open late in summer: 10:00–22:00 in July, 10:00–23:00 in August.
  • Parc National de l'Archipel de La Maddalena, journée en mer — Sardinia's first national park (1994): more than 60 islands, 180 km of coastline, over 20,000 protected hectares of land and sea, directly opposite Baja Sardinia. On the programme, depending on the wind: Budelli and its pink beach, Spargi, Santa Maria, Razzoli, Caprera and Santo Stefano. Departures by gozzo or private yacht from Baja Sardinia, Cannigione or Palau, 15–25 minutes from the hotels. Anchorages and landings are regulated by the Ente Parco (Via Giulio Cesare 7, La Maddalena, +39 0789 790211): the itinerary is set the day before according to the weather and the permits in force.
