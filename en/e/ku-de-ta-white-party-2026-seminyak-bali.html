Ku De Ta White Party 2026

Saturday 15 August 2026, from 6pm to 4am · Ku De Ta, Jalan Kayu Aya No.9, Seminyak, Bali 80361 · Palace Hotels & Fine Dining

Ku De Ta's White Party is Seminyak's all-white rendezvous, on the most sought-after beach of the west coast. The 2026 edition is themed "Atlantis – The Lost Kingdom of Light" and entrusted to the duo CHAMBORD. Dinner and cocktails facing the sunset, then ten hours of partying until four in the morning, feet in the sand of Jalan Kayu Aya. The house requires an entirely white outfit and makes it a condition of entry.

Line-up : Headliner: the duo CHAMBORD. Edition theme: "Atlantis – The Lost Kingdom of Light". Organised by: Ku De Ta Seminyak.

How to get in

Ku De Ta Seminyak — reservations office, and the official white-label Megatix ticketing platform.

Tickets on Ku De Ta's dedicated Megatix page; tables, banquettes and premium dinners through the reservations office by phone, WhatsApp or email.

The turnkey stay

On Saturday 15 August, from 6pm to 4am, Ku De Ta turns the beach of Seminyak absolute white: the White Party, the annual tradition of the house at Jalan Kayu Aya No.9, requires its signature white dress code of every guest. The 2026 edition takes the theme "Atlantis: The Lost Kingdom of Light", with the Parisian duo CHAMBORD headlining. You arrive for the sunset over the Indian Ocean, glass in hand facing the rollers, and only leave the sand in the early morning. Beforehand, on Thursday 6 August from 6pm to 9pm, the Runway To White Party parades the collections of six designers along the seafront, led by Mauricio Alpizar: the appetizer to the night Bali waits for all year.

Where to stay

  • The Oberoi Beach Resort, Bali — Five-star resort set on Jalan Kayu Aya, the very street of Ku De Ta, with direct access to 500 metres of sand on Seminyak Beach. Pavilions and villas scattered through tropical gardens, the Oberoi Spa with thatched-roof treatment rooms opening onto the gardens, restaurants Kura Kura (ocean view) and Frangipani Cafe (beachfront), Kayu Bar with its candlelit cocktails facing the sea, and a natural-stone amphitheatre carved in palace style hosting themed dinners and cultural performances.
  • The Seminyak Beach Resort & Spa — A beach resort in the heart of Seminyak, on the same shore as Ku De Ta. One- and two-bedroom villas with private pool (garden or ocean), Ocean Suites with jacuzzi, Garden and Beach rooms, an infinity pool facing the ocean, the Kahyangan spa and two restaurants, the Sanje Restaurant & Lounge for sunsets and the Santan.

Where to dine

  • La Lucciola — A beach institution for thirty years, set on the sand at the foot of the Petitenget temple (15th century), on the same coastline as Ku De Ta. Regional Italian cuisine, pasta hand-rolled before every service, warm breads from the in-house bakery; continuous service from breakfast (9am) to dinner (from 5pm) and the private room The Fire Fly Room for up to 40 covers. No Michelin star is claimed by the house.
  • Merah Putih Bali — Jl. Petitenget No.100x, Kerobokan — the street next to Jalan Kayu Aya, a stone's throw from Ku De Ta. Indonesian cuisine by Wiwik, the house's executive chef, revisiting the archipelago's classics and creations built around traditional spices. Distinguished as Best Indonesian Restaurant in Bali 2025 and Best Fine Dining Restaurant in Bali 2024. No Michelin star is claimed by the house.

What to experience

  • SGi Air Bali — transfert privé et vol panoramique en hélicoptère — The operator that opened up Bali's skies more than twenty-five years ago, based at Benoa Heliport (Denpasar): BARS Gold safety certification — the standard of the great international companies — backed by the HAI Salute to Excellence and an Operator Safety Award. Flights over Nusa Penida, the Uluwatu cliffs and Mount Batur, private VIP transfers, private charters and marriage-proposal flights. The right way to arrive in Bali — or to leave it the next day.
  • SUNDARI Wellness — A holistic spa at Jalan Petitenget 7, Kerobokan, the street next to Ku De Ta. An approach founded on the Balinese philosophy of Tri Hita Karana: massages, body treatments, healing rituals, sauna and bath rituals, and advanced-technology facials (micro-currents, collagen induction, stem cells). Open daily from 9am to 10pm — exactly what you need the day after a night that ends at 4am.
