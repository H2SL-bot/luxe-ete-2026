Festival de Cannes 2027, 80th edition

From 11 to 22 May 2027, the 80th anniversary edition. No public ticketing: accreditation and invitations. Registration for 2027 opens around early February. · Palais des Festivals et des Congrès, 1 boulevard de la Croisette, 06400 Cannes · Festivals & Music
✦✦✦✦✦ Radar score : 89/100
✓ Verified at the source on 24/08/2026

The 80th edition of the world's greatest film festival: twelve days of screenings, red-carpet ascents and seventh-art diplomacy on the Croisette. An anniversary edition whose official communication promises a celebration of the Festival's eight decades. The dates of the Marché du Film 2027 remain to be confirmed.

How to get in

Access to the Cannes Film Festival is strictly reserved for film industry professionals and accredited press. The official accreditation portal (festival-cannes.com/en/take-part/accreditations/) opens each year in early February for the Professionals, Press (print/digital and audiovisual) and Film Market categories, on presentation of proof of activity (professional card, contract, assignment letter); a mandatory environmental contribution of €20 excl. tax per person applies to every accreditation. No tickets are sold to the general public for the official evening screenings: seats go through online ticketing reserved solely for accredited attendees, and red-carpet invitations come from the distributors and producers of the selected films, who receive an allocation of seats to distribute among the film's team, partners and guests — an industry-internal mechanism, with no public named contact to approach.

The Marché du Film (marchedufilm.com), held alongside the Festival, issues paid professional badges; 2027 rates and registration dates remain to be confirmed on marchedufilm.com/registration (for reference, 2026 registration ran from 2 February to 27 April, with a price increase past a cutoff date). Accommodation in Cannes during the Festival's 12 days must be booked well in advance (6 to 12 months): Croisette hotel rates rise three- to five-fold over the period. For the general public, only certain daytime screenings linked to the parallel sections are more accessible to accredited attendees; no official seats are sold directly to the public for the gala evenings.

The turnkey stay

For the 80th anniversary of the Cannes Film Festival, in May 2027, La Croisette once again becomes the world's epicentre of glamour: legendary palace hotels, a starred restaurant and a yacht moored facing the Palais des Festivals compose the discreet stage where the Riviera's finest nights are brokered. From a suite overlooking the bay to Jean Imbert's table, by way of a private evening at sea or on a gala beach, this turnkey stay orchestrates the ultimate jet-set experience to live this historic gathering of world cinema from the wings of luxury itself.

Where to stay

  • Carlton Cannes, a Regent Hotel — The legendary 1911 palace hotel on the Croisette, a historic setting for Festival stars, fully renovated and reopened in 2023 under the IHG flag.
  • Hôtel Martinez, The Unbound Collection by Hyatt — An Art Deco palace hotel from 1929 facing the Croisette, with a private beach and emblematic suites hosting the teams of competing films each year.
  • Hôtel Barrière Le Majestic Cannes — A 1926 palace hotel right in the heart of the Croisette, facing the Palais des Festivals, with the private Ciro's beach and views of the Lérins Islands.

Where to dine

  • La Palme d'Or (Hôtel Martinez) — A Michelin-starred restaurant (1 star, 2026 edition) by chef Jean Imbert, a gourmet tribute to cinema in an Art Deco setting facing the sea.
  • BFire by Mauro Colagreco (Plage Barrière Le Majestic) — A beach restaurant by triple-starred chef Mauro Colagreco (Mirazur), wood-fire cuisine with feet in the sand on the Croisette.

What to experience

  • Yacht charter pendant le Festival de Cannes (Edmiston) — Superyacht charter moored in the bay or at the Vieux Port facing the Palais des Festivals, with a broker specialising in Festival events.
  • Plage privée Ciro's Cannes (Hôtel Barrière Le Majestic) — A gala beach facing the Lérins Islands, private pontoon, valet service and exclusive parties throughout the Festival's 12 days.
