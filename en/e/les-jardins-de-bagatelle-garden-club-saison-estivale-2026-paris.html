Les Jardins de Bagatelle — Garden Club (Summer Season 2026)

Saison estivale 2026 · soirées dansantes récurrentes du jeudi au samedi, du printemps à l'automne (dates variables, non publiées à l'avance) · Les Jardins de Bagatelle (Paris Society), lisière du Bois de Boulogne, 16e · Festivals & Music

A garden of more than 1,000 m² that, once night falls, turns into an open-air club: dinner and then dancing under the stars, DJ sets and the chic garden-party spirit of Paris's beau monde. An enclosed leafy setting, an elegant dress code and a published table booking — the Parisian answer to the 'beach club in the city', where a place can be reserved.

Line-up : Resident DJ on summer evenings (no dated headliner announced — unverifiable)

How to get in

Paris Society

Table booking / dinner-and-evening via published public platforms (access.sb, clubbable) or direct contact with the venue; door policy and elegant attire required

The turnkey stay

On the edge of the Bois de Boulogne, in the most hushed reaches of the 16th arrondissement, the spirit of Les Jardins de Bagatelle stretches from morning to night. After the dancing beneath the trees of the Garden Club, the stay unfolds to the rhythm of the Bois's alleys: rowing on the lake at first light, contemporary art in Gehry's glass vessel, then a Michelin-starred dinner in a Belle Époque pavilion just minutes from the rose garden. A Parisian art of living in which the party never quite leaves the garden.

Where to stay

  • Saint James Paris — Paris's only château-hotel, a 5-star Relais & Châteaux and private club, on the very edge of the Bois de Boulogne (5 place du Chancelier Adenauer, 16e). 56 rooms and suites, a 400 m² Spa Guerlain with pool and the gastronomic table Bellefeuille — the address closest to Bagatelle.
  • The Peninsula Paris — A palace hotel at 19 avenue Kléber (16e), a stone's throw from the Arc de Triomphe. 200 rooms and suites, the largest luxury-hotel spa in Paris, and the Michelin-starred rooftop L'Oiseau Blanc with views of the Tour Eiffel.

Where to dine

  • Le Pré Catelan — Frédéric Anton's three-Michelin-star table, at the heart of the Bois de Boulogne — the gastronomic table closest to Bagatelle. High precision in a Belle Époque pavilion, with a cellar of more than a thousand vintages and champagnes.
  • La Grande Cascade — A Michelin-starred institution of the Bois de Boulogne (allée de Longchamp), a former hunting lodge of Napoleon III turned Art nouveau rotunda for the 1900 World's Fair. Cuisine by chef Frédéric Robert, terrace beneath the foliage.

What to experience

  • Fondation Louis Vuitton — Frank Gehry's glass vessel, on the edge of the Jardin d'Acclimatation in the Bois de Boulogne (8 avenue du Mahatma Gandhi, 16e). Leading exhibitions of modern and contemporary art; open every day except Tuesday, late opening on Friday.
  • Balade en barque, Chalet des Îles — Rowing-boat hire on the Lac inférieur of the Bois de Boulogne, setting off from the Porte de la Muette. A lap of the island in an hour's rowing, spring–summer 2026 season — the ideal bucolic escape to prolong the garden spirit of the evening.
