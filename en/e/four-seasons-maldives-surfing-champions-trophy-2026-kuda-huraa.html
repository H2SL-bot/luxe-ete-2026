Four Seasons Maldives Surfing Champions Trophy 2026

From 4 to 11 September 2026 · Four Seasons Resort Maldives at Kuda Huraa · Other

The most exclusive gathering in world surfing: a handful of champions, all invited, compete across three board formats — single fin, twin fin and thruster — before a grand final. Held since 2011 under the banner of the Four Seasons Kuda Huraa, the event blends exceptional competition with palace-level hospitality, far from circuits open to the public. Kai Lenny, Mason Ho and Victor Bernardo are confirmed for the 2026 edition.

Line-up : Kai Lenny, presented by the organisers as one of the world's greatest watermen, honoured at the WSL Big Wave Awards (Men's Best Overall Performance and Men's XXL Biggest Wave), Mason Ho, "The World's Most Entertaining Surfer", and Victor Bernardo, confirmed by the organisers on 8 July 2026. Event held in association with Tropicsurf.

How to get in

Four Seasons Resort Maldives at Kuda Huraa, with Tropicsurf as surf partner. The field of athletes is assembled exclusively by invitation from the organisers.

Book the official trophy week package through the Reservations team at the Four Seasons Kuda Huraa, by telephone or email — accommodation and package requests go through the same channel, listed as "Accommodation & Package Enquiries" on the official website.
