Ravello Festival – Closing Night with Sir Simon Rattle

Saturday 5 September 2026 · Belvedere di Villa Rufolo · Festivals & Music
✦✦✦✦ Radar score : 76/100

The 74th edition draws to a close with the Freiburger Barockorchester conducted by Sir Simon Rattle — one of the most revered conductors in the world — for the last great night of the Amalfi season. The international set's end-of-summer rendezvous before the grand hotels close their seasons.

Line-up : Sir Simon Rattle, Freiburger Barockorchester

How to get in

Fondazione Ravello, a musical and cultural body under the aegis of the Campania Region, organiser of the Ravello Festival (74th edition, 2026) on the Belvedere of the Villa Rufolo.

Ticket at 60 EUR via the official ticketing (ravellofestival.com or the Piazza Duomo box office); the closing evening of the 74th edition with Sir Simon Rattle, in high demand — book early. Under 26 discount (20 EUR on site). Free avenue: press accreditation via the ufficio stampa (ufficiostampa@fondazioneravello.it) or Lucia Serino (l.serino@fondazioneravello.it).

The turnkey stay

On 5 September 2026 at 7:30pm, the Belvedere of Villa Rufolo closes the 74th edition of the Ravello Festival with a grand finale by Sir Simon Rattle: the Freiburger Barockorchester, playing period instruments, accompanies violinist Isabelle Faust in a programme devoted entirely to Schumann. An evening of open-air musical craftsmanship, the summer highlight of a festival born in Wagner's wake. The setting — a vertiginous drop over the Gulf of Salerno — makes it one of the most sought-after dates on the European classical scene.

Where to stay

  • Palazzo Avino — 12th-century palace in the centre of Ravello, a few minutes' walk from Villa Rufolo, renowned for its panoramic terraces and high-end service.
  • Caruso, A Belmond Hotel, Amalfi Coast — Former 11th-century palace converted into a 5-star hotel, with an infinity pool suspended 350 m above the sea.

Where to dine

  • Rossellinis — Starred restaurant at the Palazzo Avino, light and elegant regional cuisine served on a terrace overlooking the Amalfi Coast.

What to experience

  • Duomo di Ravello e Museo del Duomo — Santa Maria Assunta e San Pantaleone Cathedral, dating from the 11th century, with a museum housing sculptures, silverware and ancient sarcophagi in the crypt.
