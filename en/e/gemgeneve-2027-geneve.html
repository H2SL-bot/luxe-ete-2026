GemGenève 2027

From 11 to 14 May 2027 at Palexpo, 11th edition. Open to trade and enthusiasts alike; 2027 ticketing is on its way ('Ticketing soon'). · Palexpo, route François-Peyrot 30, 1218 Le Grand-Saconnex (Genève) · Jewellery & Watchmaking
✦✦ Radar score : 55/100
✓ Verified at the source on 24/08/2026

The Geneva gem and jewellery fair, founded by dealers for dealers and now the spring rendezvous of connoisseurs: exceptional stones, historic traders, independent jewellers and young designers, in an atmosphere closer to a cabinet of curiosities than to a commercial fair. 11th edition.

How to get in

No dedicated VIP/collector channel published. Access via the MyBadgeOnline visitor/trade badge (gemgeneve.mybadgeonline.com/Visiteur-en-US), ID required on collection. Press: accreditation via gemgeneve.mybadgeonline.com/Presse or press@gemgeneve.com; press contacts by country listed at gemgeneve.com/press-material/. Exhibitors: gemgeneve.com/exhibitors/.

2027 edition: ticketing not yet open, the gemgeneve.com/practical/ page shows 'Ticketing soon' (previous-edition reference: paid visitor badge CHF 50 incl. tax). Confirmed 2027 hours: Tue 11, Wed 12 and Thu 13 May 10am-6:30pm, Fri 14 May 10am-5pm (last entry 4pm). Venue: Palexpo Hall 2, route François-Peyrot 30, 1218 Le Grand-Saconnex, Geneva.

The turnkey stay

During GemGenève, gemmologists, jewellers and collectors from around the world converge on Palexpo. This turnkey stay is anchored on the shore of Lake Geneva, between a historic palace hotel and starred gastronomy, just minutes from the airport and the fair venue. Evenings extend along the lakeshore or in the hushed salons of Geneva's grand hotels, with the chance to pair fair days with the city's essential watchmaking and lakeside experiences: the Patek Philippe Museum, a Belle Époque cruise, signature restaurants.

Where to stay

  • Hôtel Beau-Rivage Genève — An independent family-run palace hotel since 1865 on the shores of Lake Geneva, a historic reference of luxury hospitality in Geneva.
  • Mandarin Oriental, Genève — A contemporary palace hotel on the banks of the Rhône, a stone's throw from the quays and the city's financial and jewellery district.

Where to dine

  • Le Chat-Botté (Hôtel Beau-Rivage) — The Beau-Rivage's historic gastronomic restaurant, one Michelin star; reopening announced for 2027 after renovation.
  • SACHI (Mandarin Oriental, Genève) — A gastronomic Japanese restaurant with a 10-seat omakase bar, opened in 2022, with a chef trained by Nobu Matsuhisa.

What to experience

  • Visite du Musée Patek Philippe — A landmark collection of watchmaking and art in Geneva, spanning five centuries of the Geneva art of timekeeping; guided tours available.
  • Croisière Belle Époque CGN sur le lac Léman — A cruise aboard the CGN's historic steamboats, with lunch or gastronomic dinner formulas on the lake.
