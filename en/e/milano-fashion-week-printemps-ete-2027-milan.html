Milano Fashion Week — Spring/Summer 2027

22-28 September 2026 · Milan (various venues) · Divers lieux (Camera Nazionale della Moda Italiana) · Fashion & Couture
✓ Verified at the source on 16/07/2026

Prada, Gucci, Versace, Armani for the finale: the Italian fashion capital rolls out its exceptional red carpet. The summit of Italian luxury, where the greatest houses close the week with spectacular runway shows.

Line-up : Italian houses, front-row celebrities, international press

How to get in

Camera Nazionale della Moda Italiana (CNMI), organiser of Milano Fashion Week (Milano Moda Donna, Spring-Summer 2027 collections)

The shows are by invitation from the houses: no public ticketing. Realistic professional avenue: press accreditation with the CNMI (form at cameramoda.it/en/area-stampa/stampa-accrediti/, fee ~50 EUR, QR-code badge), granting access to the list of press offices of the brands on the calendar and to the official shuttles. For a front-row seat: go through the press officer of the house in question. Other options: PR via specialised agencies or concierge services, and presentations/showrooms open by registration during the week.

The turnkey stay

From 22 to 28 September 2026, Milan unveils the Spring-Summer 2027 collections: some sixty shows orchestrated by the Camera Nazionale della Moda Italiana, Prada raising the curtain on 22 September, Vogue World: Milano opening the week and a gala dinner at the Palazzo Reale on the 23rd. The Quadrilatero della Moda then becomes the private drawing room of the international jet set: you stay between via Gesù and via Montenapoleone, a few steps from the fashion houses' presentations. And when the calendar allows, you treat yourself to a fifteen-minute helicopter hop for lunch on Lake Como before the evening show.

Where to stay

  • Four Seasons Hotel Milano — A former 15th-century convent on via Gesù, in the very heart of the Quadrilatero della Moda: via Montenapoleone is a two-minute walk away, La Scala seven. 118 keys including 50 suites around the Renaissance cloister, a spa designed by Patricia Urquiola with seven treatment rooms and a pool — the address closest to the fashion houses' salons during Fashion Week.
  • Bvlgari Hotel Milano — An 18th-century Milanese palazzo on a private lane adjoining via Montenapoleone, turned inward around a 4,000 m² private garden bordering the Brera botanical garden. Its landmark urban spa, with a pool in gold and green mosaic, is the place to catch your breath between two shows without leaving the fashion district.

Where to dine

  • Seta by Antonio Guida — Two Michelin stars at the Mandarin Oriental, in the La Scala district, at the gates of the Quadrilatero. Chef Antonio Guida, supported by Federico Dell'Omarino and pastry chef Marco Pinna, serves his Italian haute cuisine in the hotel's second glass-roofed courtyard, opened to the outside in the warmer months.
  • Cracco in Galleria — One Michelin star beneath the glass vaults of the Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II, between the Duomo and La Scala. Carlo Cracco, with his resident chef Luca Sacchi, revisits the Milanese classics — vitello alla milanese, open raviolo — and his ground-floor café-patisserie is the natural post-show meeting point.

What to experience

  • Transfert privé en hélicoptère vers le lac de Côme, Helitaly — The insiders' escape between two shows: about 15 minutes' flight from Milan (departures from Linate, Malpensa or the operator's helipads) to the grand hotels of Lake Como, aboard aircraft from certified operators chartered by Helitaly.
  • Motoscafo privé en acajou sur le lac, Barindelli Taxi Boat — A private cruise aboard a Venetian-style taxi boat in varnished mahogany, departing from the private landing stages of Bellagio (Piazza Mazzini) and Varenna: a tour of the lake's villas from one hour to a full day, Lombard art de vivre in its most photogenic form.
