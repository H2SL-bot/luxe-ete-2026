Notti Toscane 2026 — Andrea Bocelli gala (Alpemare & Villa Alpebella)

26–27 July 2026 (two gala evenings) · Bagno Alpemare, Forte dei Marmi (26 juil.) & Villa Alpebella, Poveromo près de Forte dei Marmi (27 juil.) · Festivals & Music

Two charity gala nights hosted by Andrea Bocelli and the ABF Foundation. Night one: sunset aperitivo followed by a beachside dinner with live music at Alpemare Beach Club (beach cocktail dress code). Night two: gourmet dinner and gala concert at the Maestro's private villa, with international guest artists (gala attire). Genuine but selective access: participation is secured through a charitable contribution to the Andrea Bocelli Foundation (via Celebrity Adventures), not through open ticket sales.

Line-up : Andrea Bocelli and international guest artists (full line-up not published)

How to get in

By invitation through a charitable donation

Contact official organiser Sean Currie (Celebrity Adventures) to become a supporter of the Andrea Bocelli Foundation and receive the invitation — not available through open ticket sales.

The turnkey stay

Two late-July nights when the Versilia plays its rarest score: on the 26th, a gala dinner on the sand at Bagno Alpemare, the Bocelli family's beach club, aperitivo at sunset then music facing the Tyrrhenian; on the 27th, the gates of Villa Alpebella open onto the Maestro's private garden for a gourmet dinner and a recital a few metres from the sea. In between, you live Forte dei Marmi like a regular: a cabana in the shade of the pines in the morning, Carrara marble and the Apuan Alps in the afternoon, fish suppers at night, all just a few hundred metres from the two gala venues. A three-night stay (25–28 July), chauffeured car for the evening transfers, white linen de rigueur.

Where to stay

  • Principe Forte dei Marmi — A palace hotel and member of The Leading Hotels of the World, Viale Ammiraglio Morin 67 — the same seafront avenue as Bagno Alpemare (Viale Italico 69), a few minutes' walk from the gala on the 26th. Suites in signature Italian design, Egoista Spa, the private Dalmazia beach, the 67 Sky Lounge rooftop looking out on the Apuan Alps and the Michelin-starred Lux Lucis by chef Valentino Cassanelli. The ideal base for walking home after dinner on the sand. Tel. +39 0584 783636.
  • Augustus Hotel & Resort — The historic five-star address of Forte dei Marmi, Viale Morin 169: a private park of umbrella pines, several villas including Villa Agnelli, two heated pools (one seafront with jacuzzi), day spa, fitness, two restaurants and a boat service. Its signature: the underground tunnel linking the park straight to the private Augustus Lido beach, without ever crossing the road. Tel. +39 0584 787200.

Where to dine

  • Bistrot — Forte dei Marmi — The benchmark fine-dining table of Forte dei Marmi, run by the Vaiani family and starred in the Michelin guide: Versilian seafood cooking poised “between authentic flavours and experimentation”, in an intimate dining room with impeccable service. Book it for dinner on the evening of the 25th, to open the stay before the two gala nights. Reservations via the Contatti page of the website.
  • Ristorante Lorenzo — A seafood institution in Forte dei Marmi since 1981, Via Giosuè Carducci 61, right in the centre a step from the market. The pescato del giorno presented at the table, a wine list saluted by Le Soste, Gambero Rosso, L'Espresso and Bibenda, and the ritual of the late lunch so dear to Forte dei Marmi regulars. Perfect for lunch on the 27th, before the evening at Villa Alpebella.

What to experience

  • Bagno Alpemare — journée en cabine privée (plage & mer) — The Bocelli family's beach club, Viale Italico 69, voted Best Beach Club Italy 2024 and 2025: 120 luxury cabanas facing the Tyrrhenian, the Alpemare and Marecalmo restaurants, bar and aperitivo. Enjoy it by day, sun lounger and lunch with your feet in the sand, on the very site of the gala dinner on the evening of 26 July. Beach reservations online, tel. +39 0584 1811042 / info@alpemare.com.
  • Cave di Marmo Tours — carrières de marbre de Carrare (formule Gold ou Diamond) — An excursion into the heart of the Apuan Alps quarries, departing from Colonnata (Strada Comunale per Colonnata 1), about 30 minutes from Forte dei Marmi. The Gold formula in a private vehicle with a dedicated guide, or bespoke Diamond for a special occasion: you come into direct contact with statuary marble, Michelangelo's own, before heading back down to lunch on lardo di Colonnata. Reservation required, tel. +39 0585 625112 / info@cavedimarmotours.com.
