Ravello Festival – "Götterdämmerung" conducted by Kent Nagano

Friday 31 July 2026 · Belvedere di Villa Rufolo (scène suspendue sur la mer) · Festivals & Music
✦✦✦ Radar score : 66/100

An exceptional Wagnerian evening of the 74th edition: Twilight of the Gods in concert performance by the Dresdner Festspielorchester and Concerto Köln under the baton of Kent Nagano, on the most spectacular belvedere in the Mediterranean. The most coveted date of the Amalfi Coast's musical summer, treasured by the music-loving elite in residence at the Caruso and Palazzo Avino.

Line-up : Kent Nagano, Dresdner Festspielorchester, Concerto Köln

How to get in

Fondazione Ravello, a musical and cultural body under the aegis of the Campania Region, organiser of the Ravello Festival (74th edition, 2026) on the Belvedere of Villa Rufolo.

Ticket at 60 EUR via the official box office (ravellofestival.com or the Piazza Duomo box office); limited seats on the Belvedere of Villa Rufolo, book early. Under 26 discount (20 EUR on site, excluding the Concerto all'alba). Free route: press accreditation via the press office (ufficiostampa@fondazioneravello.it) or Lucia Serino (l.serino@fondazioneravello.it). Music-loving audiences stay at the Caruso Belmond and the Palazzo Avino.

The turnkey stay

On 31 July 2026, the suspended Belvedere of Villa Rufolo becomes the setting for an absolute Wagnerian shock: the Dresdner Festspielorchester and Concerto Köln, conducted by Kent Nagano, perform a concert version of the Prologue and Act I of Götterdämmerung, as part of "The Wagner Cycles" series of the Dresdner Musikfestspiele. It was here, in the gardens of Rufolo, that Wagner himself found the "magic garden of Klingsor" that inspired Parsifal — a circle that is both jet-set and historic. An evening for discerning music lovers, between the sheer drop over the Gulf of Salerno and open-air orchestral intensity.

Where to stay

  • Caruso, A Belmond Hotel, Amalfi Coast — 11th-century palace perched 350 m above the sea, with a legendary infinity pool and terraced gardens of vines and lemon trees; a few minutes' walk from the Belvedere of Villa Rufolo.
  • Palazzo Avino — Former 12th-century palace converted into a 5-star hotel in the heart of Ravello, with panoramic terraces over the Amalfi Coast and renowned jet-set service.

Where to dine

  • Rossellinis — 1-Michelin-star gastronomic restaurant at the Palazzo Avino, refined regional cuisine by Gianni Vanacore, served on a terrace suspended above the coast.

What to experience

  • Villa Rufolo — 13th-century villa and gardens whose Belvedere hosts the Ravello Festival; the site that inspired Richard Wagner for the garden of Klingsor in Parsifal.
