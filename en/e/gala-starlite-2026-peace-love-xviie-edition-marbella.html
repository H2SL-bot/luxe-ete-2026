Starlite Gala 2026 “Peace & Love” (17th edition)

Sunday 9 August 2026 · Auditorio Starlite, Cantera de Nagüeles, Calle Albinoni s/n, 29602 Marbella · Festivals & Music

Seventeenth edition of the Costa del Sol's most sought-after charity gala, held this year under the theme “Peace & Love”. The evening takes place in the former Nagüeles quarry, a natural amphitheatre ringed by high rock walls, transformed every summer into an open-air auditorium. Founded in 2010 and led by Sandra García-Sanjuán and Antonio Banderas, the gala donates its proceeds to charitable causes and draws the Spanish and international elite of cinema, culture and business every year.

Line-up : Sandra García-Sanjuán and Antonio Banderas, founders and hosts of the gala; Spanish and international figures from cinema, culture and business.

How to get in

Fundación Starlite, chaired by Sandra García-Sanjuán, with Antonio Banderas as host of the evening.

Individual tickets and patron tables sold by the Fundación Starlite; 2026 ticketing had not yet opened at the last check (“Próximamente”). Register by email or telephone with the Foundation.

The turnkey stay

On 9 August at 8 pm, the Cantera de Nagüeles — a former quarry turned open-air amphitheatre, ringed by sixty-metre rock walls at the foot of the Sierra Blanca — hosts the 17th Starlite Gala, orchestrated as every summer by Sandra García-Sanjuán and Antonio Banderas. Under the banner “Peace & Love”, the auditorium's three thousand five hundred seats make up the most sought-after charity event of the Andalusian season: the Starlite Gala has raised more than four million euros over its editions. It is the high point of a Starlite summer of forty-nine concert nights, from 19 June to 29 August, and the one evening when the entire Golden Mile climbs up to the quarry, in evening gowns beneath the stars. Note: you park below and shuttles run up to the auditorium.

Where to stay

  • Marbella Club Hotel, Golf Resort & Spa — Marbella's founding palace hotel, born in 1954 under the impetus of Prince Alfonso von Hohenlohe, Bulevar Príncipe Alfonso von Hohenlohe, about 3 km below the Cantera de Nagüeles — less than ten minutes by car to the auditorium. Rooms, suites, bungalows and villas (up to four bedrooms) scattered through vast gardens. The spa places thalassotherapy at the heart of its philosophy, around a seawater pool, and offers treatments, meditation, Pilates and nutrition programmes. Ten dining venues and bars, including El Patio, The Grill, El Olivar, La Bodega, Rudi's and the beach club MC Beach. Private beach, tennis and golf. Member of the Leading Hotels of the World.
  • Puente Romano Beach Resort, Marbella — On the same Bulevar Príncipe Alfonso von Hohenlohe, on the Nagüeles beachfront — the very neighbourhood that gives the quarry its name — about 3 km and less than ten minutes from the Auditorio Starlite. More than twenty restaurants and bars, including Nobu, Cipriani, COYA, Sea Grill, La Petite Maison, GAIA and the steakhouse Leña by Dani García. The Six Senses Spa offers Mediterranean-inspired treatments, complemented by a Technogym fitness centre; the resort's tennis club is a coastal institution. The jet set's natural base camp during the festival.

Where to dine

  • Skina — Two Michelin stars, Marbella's absolute reference table. Sommelier Marcos Granda set up his house in a former cortijo at 9 avenida Cánovas del Castillo, facing the Parque de los Enamorados, at the entrance to the Golden Mile; in the kitchen, Toledo-born chef Mario Cachinero reinterprets the classic Andalusian repertoire with a highly personal signature. Wine list of more than 6,000 references, Evolución and Grand Cru menus. Note: dinners Monday to Saturday, closed Sunday — so book for the eve of the gala, or the day after.
  • Messina — One star in the 2026 Michelin Guide. An institution opened in 2003 by chef Mauricio Giovanini, with Pía Ninci running the dining room, avenida Severo Ochoa 12, 29603 Marbella. A proudly free-spirited cuisine, nourished by flavours and nuances from around the world rather than the fashions of the moment — the ideal creative counterpoint to Skina's Andalusian classicism.

What to experience

  • HeliAir Marbella — transfert et survol en hélicoptère — An operator established in Marbella since 1988 (company iTell Aviation SL), with its own heliport at km 2 of the Carretera de Istán, 29602 Marbella, and a fleet of Bell 505, EC120 and R44. VIP transfers and private charters, scenic flights — the Puerto Banús tour is the house classic, from 900 € — and Fly & Drive packages combining a flight with a supercar on arrival. The finest way to reach the coast on gala day: see the Sierra Blanca and the Nagüeles quarry from the sky before descending to it in the evening.
  • Marbella Yacht Charter — journée privée au départ de Puerto Banús — More than fifteen high-end motor yachts departing from the Puerto Banús marina, from the SAY 40 day-cruiser to the Sunseeker Predator 72 and the Mangusta 80, via the Princess V58, the Nuvari 64 and the Windy 42 Grand Bora. Qualified professional crew included in all charters, by half-day, full day or several days, with seasonal rates. The perfect day before 9 August: anchoring off the Golden Mile, back to port in the late afternoon, up to the quarry at dusk.
