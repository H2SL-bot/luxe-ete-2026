Sotheby's Hong Kong — Modern & Contemporary Evening Auction

Evening sale on Tuesday 29 September 2026, followed by the Modern Day Auction and the Contemporary Day Auction on Wednesday 30 September 2026 · Sotheby's Maison, Landmark Chater, 8 Connaught Road Central, Central · Fashion & Couture

Since July 2024, Sotheby's has housed its Asian flagship at Landmark Chater: a two-floor, 24,000-square-foot space designed by the Rotterdam practice MVRDV. The modern and contemporary evening sale on 29 September 2026 is the peak of the Asian autumn season, ahead of the day sales the following morning. Nicolas Chow, Chairman Asia, and Masumi Shinohara, Managing Director Asia, are its leading figures.

Line-up : Nicolas Chow, Chairman Asia; Masumi Shinohara, Managing Director Asia; Jen Hua, Deputy Chairman Asia; Nick Buckley Wood, Private Sales Director Asia. Asian collectors, international dealers and the Landmark clientele.

How to get in

Sotheby's Asia client services, at Sotheby's Maison (Landmark Chater), and the specialists of the Modern and Contemporary Art department.

Registering as a bidder with Sotheby's Hong Kong (+852 2524 8121), with bank references, gives access to the room and to a paddle. The Private Sales Asia department — Nick Buckley Wood, Private Sales Director Asia, and Fusako Oshima, Specialist Private Sales, on +852 2822 5510 — arranges appointments and private access.

The turnkey stay

La grande soirée d'art moderne et contemporain de Sotheby's referme la saison des ventes de Hong Kong dans le même écrin que la vente joaillerie de septembre : l'occasion de prolonger un séjour dédié aux enchères et à la gastronomie étoilée de Central.

Where to stay

  • Mandarin Oriental, The Landmark, Hong Kong — Situé dans le même complexe Landmark Chater que Sotheby's Maison, 109 chambres, sept étoiles Michelin cumulées dans ses restaurants.
  • Four Seasons Hotel Hong Kong — À 5 minutes à pied, au sein du complexe IFC, vue Victoria Harbour.

Where to dine

  • 8½ Otto e Mezzo Bombana — 3 étoiles Michelin, chef Umberto Bombana, à Alexandra House, quelques minutes à pied de Landmark Chater.
  • Lung King Heen (Four Seasons Hong Kong) — 2 étoiles Michelin, première table cantonaise au monde étoilée.

What to experience

  • Star Ferry — Traversée mythique de Victoria Harbour depuis Central.
