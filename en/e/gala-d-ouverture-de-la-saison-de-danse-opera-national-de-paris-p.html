Opening Gala of the Dance Season — Opéra national de Paris (Palais Garnier)

Saturday, October 10, 2026, 7:30 PM · Palais Garnier, Place de l'Opéra, 75009 Paris · Festivals & Music

Season-opening evening of the Paris Opera Ballet, bringing together the entire company (from the Dance School to the star dancers): a new creation by Juliano Nuñes for Hugo Marchand and Germain Louvet, and the repertoire premiere of a work by Jean-Christophe Maillot. An evening traditionally linked to the AROP, with a gala dinner and patrons of the Palais Garnier.

Line-up : Hugo Marchand, Germain Louvet, the Paris Opera Ballet company

How to get in

AROP — Association pour le Rayonnement de l'Opéra de Paris, an association of patrons affiliated with the Opéra national de Paris

Individual tickets via operadeparis.fr's booking calendar. Privileged access to gala seats and the reception that follows through AROP membership.

The turnkey stay

For one evening, the Palais Garnier reopens its season beneath gilt and the great chandelier. From a suite overlooking the dome, walk to your box, then extend the night in the historic salons that have seen the whole of Opera Paris pass through since 1862.

Where to stay

  • InterContinental Paris Le Grand — An 1862 palace hotel directly opposite the Opéra Garnier, listed historic salons, less than two minutes' walk from the house.

Where to dine

  • Le Grand Véfour — 2 Michelin stars, Directoire salon at the Palais-Royal, a ten-minute walk from the Palais Garnier.
