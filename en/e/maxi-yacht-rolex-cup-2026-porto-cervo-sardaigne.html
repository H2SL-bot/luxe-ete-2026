Maxi Yacht Rolex Cup 2026

6-12 September 2026 · Yacht Club Costa Smeralda, Porto Cervo · Yacht Club Costa Smeralda, Porto Cervo (Sardaigne) · Polo · Motoring · Sailing

The flagship regatta of the mega-sailing yachts: the world's greatest maxis brought together by the Yacht Club Costa Smeralda and Rolex in the turquoise waters of the Costa Smeralda. A week of spectacular racing and high society at the heart of the most exclusive club in the Mediterranean.

Line-up : International maxi yacht owners, YCCS members, Rolex ambassadors

Access

Regattas watched from the quays of Porto Cervo; hospitality, parties and prize-givings reserved for YCCS members, crews and Rolex guests.

The turnkey stay

A week in Porto Cervo to the rhythm of the Maxi Yacht Rolex Cup: palace hotels of the Costa Smeralda, Michelin-starred tables overlooking the Old Port and private sailing to La Maddalena.

Where to stay

  • Hotel Cala di Volpe, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Costa Smeralda — A 1960s icon on its own cove, with pastel facades, a private beach and suites opening onto the Gulf of Cala di Volpe.
  • Cervo Hotel, Costa Smeralda Resort — In the heart of the village, steps from the marina and the Piazzetta; contemporary Sardinian design and a private beach reached by sea shuttle.

Where to dine

  • Italo Bassi ConFusion Restaurant — The Costa Smeralda's only Michelin star, on the Promenade du Port; signature cuisine, open until 29 September 2026.
  • Finger's Porto Cervo — Roberto Okabe's creative Japanese cuisine on one of the most coveted terraces, facing the Porto Vecchio.

What to experience

  • Yacht Charter Porto Cervo – journée en yacht privé — A private crewed yacht day departing from the marina, bound for the La Maddalena archipelago and southern Corsica.
  • Helitaly – hélicoptère en Sardaigne — Helicopter transfer or scenic flight over the Costa Smeralda, with drop-off possible on a yacht or at a villa.
