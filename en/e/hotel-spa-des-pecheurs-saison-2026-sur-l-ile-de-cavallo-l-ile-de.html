Hôtel & Spa des Pêcheurs — 2026 Season on the Île de Cavallo ('Billionaires' Island')

Season from 21 May to 28 September 2026 (closing date corrected: 28, not 27 September) · Seul hôtel de l'île de Cavallo, entre Corse et Sardaigne · Palace Hotels & Fine Dining

The only hotel on the Mediterranean's most discreet private island (Small Luxury Hotels of the World): the Le Puffin restaurant facing the sea, a spa, diving in the Lavezzi archipelago, access to the Sperone golf course. No major public events: Cavallo cultivates absolute seclusion — villas of Italian and French billionaires, reachable by boat only. The ultra-private port of call between Sperone and Sardinia.

Access

Booking via hoteldespecheurs.com (info@hoteldespecheurs.com). Access: the hotel's boat shuttle or private tender from Piantarella/Bonifacio; the island is private, and access beyond the hotel and marina is restricted to residents and their guests. 2026 yoga/tennis details to be confirmed directly with the hotel (not published online).

The turnkey stay

Between Corsica and Sardinia, at the heart of the Lavezzi archipelago, Cavallo — the "billionaires' island" — can only be reached by sea or by air, and is home to a single address: the Hôtel & Spa des Pêcheurs, open for the 2026 season from 28 May to 27 September. The house unfolds its beaches, spa, restaurant, bar and beach club amid a maquis of myrtle and juniper, facing waters worthy of the Seychelles. You arrive by boat from Bonifacio or, grander still, by an eight-minute helicopter flight from Figari. The Mediterranean's most confidential address must be earned: all that remains is to be invited.

Where to stay

  • Hôtel & Spa Version Maquis Citadelle — A five-star hotel facing the citadel of Bonifacio, the natural port of departure for Cavallo. A pool set upon the maquis, a spa with Biologique Recherche treatments and the restaurant La Vista opening onto the old town — the ideal stopover on the eve of the crossing.
  • Grand Hôtel de Cala Rossa & Spa Nucca — A five-star Relais & Châteaux set on the gulf of Porto-Vecchio, right at the water's edge along a long beach of fine sand. The 500 m² adults-only Spa Nucca (pool, sauna, hammam, seven treatment rooms), rooms, suites and a cabin perched in the pine grove — a Corsican family legend.

Where to dine

  • Casadelmar — Two Michelin stars since 2009: chef Fabio Bragagnolo, with the house since it opened in 2004, crafts a Mediterranean gastronomy of rare purity, facing the gulf of Porto-Vecchio, on the route de Palombaggia.
  • Stella d'Oro — A family institution in the upper town of Bonifacio since 1965, near the church of Saint-Jean-Baptiste, in an authentic old Bonifacian house. The aubergines à la bonifacienne, a recipe passed down from mother to daughter, are a ritual here before or after Cavallo.

What to experience

  • Transfert privé en hélicoptère Figari, Cavallo avec Heli Sud Corse — An eight-minute flight between Figari airport and the island of Cavallo: the most spectacular arrival there is, over the Strait of Bonifacio, right to the threshold of the Hôtel des Pêcheurs.
  • Privatisation d'un yacht avec Briséis Croisières — A private day out of Bonifacio aboard a 20-metre crewed vessel, bound for the Lavezzi islands and Cavallo: turquoise anchorages, lunch on board and golden hour beneath the cliffs.
