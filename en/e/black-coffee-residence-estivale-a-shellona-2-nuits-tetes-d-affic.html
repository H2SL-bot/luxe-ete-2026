Black Coffee — Summer Residency at Shellona (2 Headline Nights)

Upcoming headline date: Tuesday 4 August 2026 (the first date, 16 July 2026, passed on 17 July). Table reservation with an increased minimum spend on headline nights. · Shellona, plage de Pampelonne, Chemin des Tamaris · Festivals & Music

Two flagship nights from South African superstar Black Coffee (Grammy Award winner, emblematic resident of Ibiza and Mykonos) at the Shellona beach club on Pampelonne. Sunset, deep house and a champagne table: the archetype of the exceptional night out in the gulf of Saint-Tropez. Way in: a table with an increased minimum spend on headline nights (SevenRooms reservation).

Line-up : Black Coffee (DJ/producer, Grammy Award)

How to get in

Shellona St-Tropez

Table reservation via SevenRooms (official link from shellona.com); or telephone +33 4 94 43 87 34 / email pampelonne@shellonabeach.com

The turnkey stay

Two nights on the peninsula, set to Pampelonne's most beautiful hour: the one when the sand is still warm and Black Coffee takes over the decks at Shellona, chemin des Tamaris, on Tuesday 4 August 2026 — his second and final headline date of the summer, the first, on 16 July, having already passed. The stay is based in the hills of Ramatuelle, ten minutes from the beach: a nap under the pines, lunch with your feet in the sand, a Cru Classé rosé tasting at golden hour, then a haute-cuisine dinner before heading down to the sea. Table booked well in advance at Shellona — increased minimum spend on headline nights — and a valet arranged for the return, since the Tropezian art of living dictates that one never checks the time.

Where to stay

  • La Réserve Ramatuelle — Hôtel, Spa & Villas — A contemporary palace hotel set on the Ramatuelle cliffside, chemin de la Quessine, about a 10-minute drive from Shellona and Pampelonne beach. Rooms and villas facing the open sea, a 1,000 m² Nescens spa occupying an entire floor (11 treatment rooms, a 12 m indoor pool with sea view, a 30 m outdoor pool, hammam), and two in-house restaurants including the two-Michelin-starred La Voile. The ideal base camp for recovering from a headline night.
  • Villa Marie Saint-Tropez — An Italian-inspired villa nestled in a three-hectare pine grove above the bay of Pampelonne, 1100 chemin de Val de Rian in Ramatuelle. Forty-five rooms and suites, a lagoon pool carved into the rock, a Pure Altitude spa and a shuttle to Saint-Tropez. A more intimate, greener mood, a few minutes from chemin des Tamaris.

Where to dine

  • La Vague d'Or — Cheval Blanc St-Tropez — The only three-Michelin-starred restaurant on the Côte d'Azur, signed by Arnaud Donckele, at the water's edge on Bouillabaisse beach, Saint-Tropez. Sauces and jus of anthology, peninsula fish worked species by species, 19/20 in the Gault&Millau. Seasonal opening and reservations to be requested far in advance: schedule it the evening before the Black Coffee date, so nothing feels rushed.
  • Chez Camille — A Ramatuelle institution since 1953, in the secluded cove of Bonne Terrasse at the southern tip of Pampelonne. Wood-fired bouillabaisse, grilled lobster, fish landed by the fishermen of Saint-Tropez; à la carte from 25 to 89 €, Camille tasting menu at 130 €. The peninsula's moment-of-truth lunch, ten minutes from Shellona.

What to experience

  • Journée plage au Club 55 — Pampelonne — The legendary Pampelonne beach, open here since 1955, at 43 boulevard Patch in Ramatuelle: sunbed 39 €, parasol 19 €, cabana 59 €, beach service, pontoon and boat shuttle. Open daily from 10:00 to 17:00, 27 March to 1 November 2026. To be slotted in gently on Wednesday 5 August, after the Black Coffee night.
  • Visite de cave et dégustation au Château Minuty — Gassin — A family estate since 1936, Cru Classé of the Côtes de Provence, perched on the hills of Gassin with a view over the Mediterranean: 2491 route de la Berle. A guided tour of the cellar and the vines followed by a tasting of three wines, by reservation only. Best done in the late afternoon, fifteen minutes from Pampelonne, before heading back down to the beach.
