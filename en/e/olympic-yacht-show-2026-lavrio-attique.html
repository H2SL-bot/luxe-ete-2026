Olympic Yacht Show 2026

15 to 18 October 2026 · Olympic Marine, Lavrio · Polo · Motoring · Sailing

Greece's leading in-water boat show returns to the Olympic Marine marina, at the tip of Attica, for four days of yachts moored along the pontoons. Organised by ONDECK Media, publisher of Skipper ONDECK, it is the autumn gathering of Hellenic yachting: owners, shipyards, brokers and charterers from the eastern Mediterranean meet here after the Cyclades season, the fifth edition having been held in 2025.

Line-up : Organised by ONDECK Media, publisher of Skipper ONDECK; host marina Olympic Marine, in Lavrio; Greek and international shipyards, brokers and owners.

How to get in

ONDECK Media (publisher of Skipper ONDECK), at the Olympic Marine marina in Lavrio.

Watch for the opening of 2026 ticketing on the official site; bookings, stands and exhibition requests through the sales department, by email or telephone.

The turnkey stay

For four days, Olympic Marine — one of the best-equipped marinas in the Mediterranean, at the south-eastern tip of Attica — puts its fleet in the water: motor yachts, sailing yachts and catamarans discovered in their element, pontoons underfoot. The October gathering is where Greek yachting meets, some ten kilometres from Cape Sounion and its temple of Poseidon. You come to step aboard, then you weigh anchor: Kea is three hours' sailing away, and the Cyclades begin there.

Where to stay

  • Grecotel Cape Sounio — A 5-star resort set facing the temple of Poseidon, on the Athens-Sounion road, ten minutes from the port of Lavrio — the address closest to the show. Bungalows, suites and villas with private pools tiered down towards the sea, three private beaches on the Aegean, eight restaurants and bars, and a spa redesigned around the holistic "Elevations of Wellness" programme created by Vicky Vlachonis. Selected by Tatler for its 101 Best Hotels in the World 2026.
  • Grand Resort Lagonissi — A 5-star resort spread over a private 72-acre peninsula on the Athenian Riviera, facing the Saronic Gulf. Allow about thirty kilometres by road to the port of Lavrio, and about twenty minutes to Athens airport. Sea-view rooms and beachfront bungalows, villas with private pools, private beaches, spa and wellness. Member of The Leading Hotels of the World.

Where to dine

  • Pelagos — Four Seasons Astir Palace Hotel Athens — One Michelin star awarded in 2021, under chef Luca Piscazzi. Mediterranean cuisine turned towards the sea and seasonal produce, on the Vouliagmeni peninsula — at the far end of the Athenian Riviera, a good hour's drive back up the coast from Lavrio. Dinner only, Monday to Saturday, from 7pm.
  • Patio — The Margi Hotel, Vouliagmeni — One Michelin star and a Green Star, just nine tables, in the patio of The Margi hotel in Vouliagmeni. Chef Panagiotis Giakalis serves contemporary Greek cuisine built on produce from the house's organic farm. Dinner Tuesday to Sunday, 7pm to 10.30pm. Like Pelagos, it is an evening to plan for: Vouliagmeni is at the opposite end of the Riviera from Lavrio.

What to experience

  • Navigation privée vers les Cyclades — base Istion Yachting Lavrio — A charter base set on the port of Lavrio itself (Akti Andreas Papandreou), half an hour from Athens airport: recent sailing yachts and catamarans, bareboat or with a skipper. From there, Kea is three hours' sailing away, followed by Kythnos, Serifos, Sifnos and Milos. The temple of Poseidon can also be reached by sea.
  • Spa de Cape Sounio — programme « Elevations of Wellness » — The resort's redesigned spa and the holistic programme devised by osteopath Vicky Vlachonis, structured in four progressive levels of treatment. Panoramic treatment pool facing the temple of Poseidon, a Vicky Vlachonis menu inspired by the Greek diet, and the resort's signature walk — gentle exercise, stretching and meditation facing the sea.
