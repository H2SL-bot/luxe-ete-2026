Le Bâoli Cannes — Summer Season 2026 (club & festive dinner)

Summer season 2026, open in the evenings throughout the summer (indicative dates, recurring, undated) · Le Bâoli, Port Pierre Canto, boulevard de la Croisette, 06400 Cannes · Festivals & Music
✦✦✦✦ Radar score : 79/100

A jet-set institution of the Croisette at the far end of Port Canto, Le Bâoli moves from restaurant dinner to nightclub in a setting worthy of an Indonesian palace facing the Mediterranean (up to around 700 seated covers). Resident DJs and European guests, bottle tables and guest list. It is one of the historic spots through which the society and celebrity crowd of the Festival and of the Cannes summer parades.

Line-up : No named or confirmed international headliner. Resident DJs + European guests (e.g. Goraab). Recurring celebrity/jet-set clientele (an iconic Festival de Cannes spot), but a society crowd rather than a verifiable programmed artist. Access by table/dinner.

How to get in

Bâoli Group

Table/dinner reservation through the official portal booking.baolicannes.com (Reservation button on baolicannes.com) — max 14 days in advance, up to 25 people; beyond that by email reservations@baolicannes.com or by telephone. The club cannot be booked separately (access after dinner).

The turnkey stay

The Cannes summer plays out at the eastern tip of the Croisette, where the yachts of Port Pierre Canto almost touch the umbrella pines. Here you take on the rhythm of the South: a siesta in the shade of an Art Deco palace hotel, lunch at the water's edge on an island reachable only by sea, then a late dinner at Le Bâoli before the night tips over into the club. A stay in which luxury is never demonstrative, only self-evident.

Where to stay

  • Hôtel Martinez, in The Unbound Collection by Hyatt — A legendary palace hotel dating from 1929, at 73 boulevard de la Croisette — a few minutes' walk from Port Canto. Art Deco façade, penthouses facing the bay, L'Oasis spa, private beach and the house's own starred restaurant. The closest address to Le Bâoli among the palace hotels of Cannes.
  • Carlton Cannes, a Regent Hotel — 58 boulevard de la Croisette. The 1913 icon, entirely reimagined by Tristan Auer and reopened in 2023: 332 rooms, suites and residences, a patio garden of 22,000 plants, the largest infinity pool in Cannes, a spa and Carlton Beach Club.

Where to dine

  • La Palme d'Or, Hôtel Martinez — One star in the Guide MICHELIN, Mediterranean cuisine signed by Jean Imbert: fish served raw or grilled over a wood fire, terrace plunging towards the Croisette. Special menus on summer fireworks evenings (22 July, 4, 15 and 24 August).
  • La Guérite, Île Sainte-Marguerite — A Mediterranean table and a 1,000 m² private beach on the island of Sainte-Marguerite, reachable only by sea. Boat shuttle from the harbour master's office at Port Canto — the same quay as Le Bâoli. Cuisine by chef Yiannis Kioroglou, lunches from April to October, dinners on Thursdays, Fridays and Saturdays in summer. Reservation essential.

What to experience

  • Île Saint-Honorat, vignoble et caveau de l'Abbaye de Lérins — Twenty minutes by boat from Cannes: a monastic island where twenty-one Cistercian monks have tended the vines for sixteen centuries. Tasting of the cuvées and liqueurs in the cellar, a walk between the rows facing the sea, sung offices in the church open to visitors. The perfect counterpoint to a night at Le Bâoli.
  • La Plage du Martinez, plage privée sur la Croisette — The palace hotel's historic beach, a limited number of loungers facing the bay, lunch with your feet in the sand and a beach club in the afternoon. Booking a mattress is indispensable in high season.
