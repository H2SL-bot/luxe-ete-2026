Proper attire is required, and the venue reserves the right to deny entry at the doorman’s discretion. Prohibited Attire: • Tank tops • Sleeveless t-shirts • Shorts • Excessively baggy clothing • Sandals, flip-flops, or dirty athletic sneakers • Flats (for ladies) • Beach attire • Extra baggy tees • Jerseys or athletic wear • Hats For gentlemen, it’s advisable to wear a collared shirt and pants, as shorts and sleeveless shirts are forbidden. Ladies should opt for appropriate attire, with heels being required; flats, sandals, and beachwear are not permitted. To ensure a smooth entry, guests are encouraged to dress to impress and adhere closely to these guidelines. See our [calendar](https://www.livnightclub.com/miami/events/) for upcoming events. AGE REQUIREMENTS Guests must be 21 years of age or older to enter. A valid, physical government-issued photo ID is required—photos or digital copies will not be accepted. Acceptable forms of ID include a U.S. driver’s license or any passport (U.S. or international) recognized by U.S. authorities. Non-U.S. residents must present a valid passport. Foreign government-issued IDs (other than passports) will not be accepted. WHAT TYPE OF MUSIC IS FEATURED AT LIV? LIV Nightclub in Miami Beach offers a dynamic music experience that varies throughout the week, featuring genres such as electronic dance music (EDM), hip-hop, and Top 40 hits. The specific music format depends on the night and the performing artists. For instance, during Miami Music Week 2025, LIV hosted performances by artists like Tiësto, Black Coffee, Axwell, Kaskade, Shimza, and 50 Cent, showcasing a blend of EDM and hip-hop. Similarly, during Art Basel 2024, LIV featured an impressive lineup that included Rick Ross, Offset, Diplo, DJ Khaled, John Summit, and Lil Wayne. These events highlight LIV’s commitment to bringing world-class talent and diverse musical experiences to its guests. To get the most accurate and up-to-date information on the music genre or lineup for a particular night, it’s advisable to check LIV’s official [event calendar](https://www.livnightclub.com/miami/events/).
